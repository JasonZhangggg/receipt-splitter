How it works

  1. Snap the receiptTake a photo or pick a screenshot. SplitWith reads every item and price.
  2. Share one linkFriends open it in any browser and tap what they had.
  3. Get paid backTax and tip split fairly. Each friend pays you on Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No ads, and no accounts for you or your friends.
Can I use a photo I already took?
Yes. Camera roll photos and screenshots of online orders both work.
What if an item is read wrong?
Tap it to fix it. SplitWith shows any gap between the items and the subtotal.
Other currencies?
Yes. Tap the total to change it.
