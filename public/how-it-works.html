SplitWith

A free receipt splitter. Take a photo of the receipt. Friends tap what they had and pay you back.

Split a receipt

No app and no sign-up.

How it works

  1. Take a photo of the receipt, or pick a screenshot. SplitWith reads every item and price.
  2. Send the link to the table.
  3. Each friend taps what they had. Tax and tip are shared in proportion to what each person had.
  4. They pay you back on Venmo, Cash App, PayPal or Zelle with one tap.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads and no accounts, for you or your friends.
Do my friends need an app?
No. The link opens in any phone browser.
Can I split a receipt from an image I already have?
Yes. A photo from your camera roll or a screenshot of an online order works the same as a new photo.
What if it misreads an item?
Tap the item to fix it. SplitWith compares the items with the receipt's subtotal and shows any difference.
Does it work in other currencies?
Yes. Tap the total to pick the receipt's currency.
