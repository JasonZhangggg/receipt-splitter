# B2 design

B2 is the redesign of the Split page and home that the operator settled on 2026-10-14, on the claude.ai design canvas (artboards "B2 · Sheets" and "B2 · Start a split"). This folder is the spec the B2 plan builds from. It is a static mock, not app code.

- `split.dc.html` and `home.dc.html` are the mock sources. Each holds the markup, the CSS (every token, size and motion value) and a `class Component extends DCLogic` block with the behavior. They need the canvas runtime to render, so read them as source and use the screens to see them.
- `screens/` holds 390 x 844 renders at 2x of every state. `split-*` is the Split page, `home-*` is home.
- The `.mp4` files show the motion at real speed: icon motions, delete then revert, the Payments ring opening, and Revert in History.

## Decisions

The operator made these calls while iterating. They win over older design docs and over any numbered rule they contradict. The B2 plan updates those rules.

### Everywhere

1. Minimal black and white. Geist type, thin gray rules, underlined text links for secondary actions, black pill buttons for the main action. Hierarchy comes from weight, size and spacing.
2. Color carries state only. Red marks a problem (a split over or under its price, the reason Create is blocked) and the trash icon while pressed. Green marks "Paid" in the Payments sheet only. The footer's "✓ Paid" pill is neutral (operator, 2026-10-15). The trash icon is gray at rest.
3. Icons come from heroicons-animated (MIT), outline, one 1.2 px stroke on screen at every size (operator, 2026-10-17; the stroke-width on the 24-unit grid is 1.2 × 24 ÷ the icon size, set in `icon-glyphs.ts`). Each plays a short motion on tap on the phone and on hover with a mouse. A press right after a hover lets the hover's motion finish. Status icons (the tick, the warning) play when they appear. Reduced motion turns every icon motion off.
4. The theme toggle is a sun/moon button. The icons swap with a rotate and scale, and the new theme spreads from the button as a circle (View Transitions API, with a color wipe as the fallback, instant under reduced motion).
5. Sheets slide up from the bottom and can be dragged: pull up for a tall sheet (94% of the screen), pull down past about 110 px to close. The sheet title stays pinned while the list scrolls, and a pull down from the top of a scrolled-to-top list closes the sheet as on iOS (operator, 2026-10-17). Rounded top corners, a gray hairline border, and a light dim over the whole page behind, header and footer included (operator, 2026-10-15; rule 92). The dim fades in with the sheet, follows a drag, and is gone when the sheet has left. A tap on it closes the sheet.
6. In the app, an action applies the moment it is tapped. A button that leaves the screen finishes its icon motion as it exits. The mock's 0.4 to 0.56 s wait before delete and revert is a mock shortcut, not the spec.

### Split page

7. Header: the Split's name, then the theme toggle and the share icon on the right. Under it an intro line ("Sam paid $135.83 on Saturday, Oct 11. Tap the dot next to what you had. Tap a line to edit it or split it another way.").
    The header row scrolls away with the list. Nothing on the page is pinned except the footer (operator, 2026-10-16, replacing the pinned header of 2026-10-15).
8. Receipt, People, Payments and History are underlined text links under the intro. People, Payments and History each open their own sheet. Receipt opens the full-screen photo straight away, with no sheet first (operator, 2026-10-17). There is no menu button and no sideways row of people chips.
9. Item rows: the dot toggles the claim; the rest of the row opens the Edit item sheet. No arrow. The left column shows the name, the description, then who had it. The right column shows the full price, and under it the split line ("$4.67 yours", "$6.25 each").
    In the who-had-it line only "You" is in the strong ink; other names stay muted (operator, 2026-10-16).
10. The list reads like a receipt in three parts: the Items, then a muted charges section (Subtotal, Tax, Tip, discounts and fees) with "+ Add item, tax, tip or discount" under it, then the Total between gray rules.
11. A fixed footer shows "Your share" with the amount and a black "Pay Sam" pill. The pill opens the Pay sheet (decision 25). A payer always sees the pill: it is gray (opacity 0.28, as home's blocked Create pill) and announced as disabled until they claim something, then turns black (operator, 2026-10-15). The Host and someone viewing another person's share get no pill. The label row and the amount row sit 8 px apart (operator, 2026-10-15).
12. Edit item sheet: the title "Edit item", a trash icon and an X at the top right. Fields for Item name, Price and Description. "Who had it" as dot toggles. "How to split" as underlined options (Evenly, By shares, By amount) with a one-line hint, then each person's part, then a status line with a tick or a warning.
13. Delete: the trash icon sits beside the X, drawn smaller so the two look the same size. Its lid lifts as it is pressed. After a delete an undo bar reads "Deleted Burrata · Undo" for 4 s. With one Item left the trash is disabled, and a tap says "A split needs at least one item."
14. Add sheet: an Item, Tax, Tip or Discount chooser as underlined options, Name and Amount fields, and an "Add <kind>" pill. Item also takes How many, a compact field beside the Amount, which is the line's total price (operator 2026-10-15). Item's hint reads "Split by each person who had it." (operator 2026-10-15). On a receipt whose Items are short of the printed Subtotal, Item shows "The receipt is $X short." with an underlined "Use $X" that fills the Amount, and on a Split with tax or tip a muted note says they keep the receipt's amounts and are shared across the new item too (operator 2026-10-15). An added Item fills that shortfall first, and only the rest raises the Subtotal and Total. A price edit or a delete moves both by the full difference, and a delete that would take the Total under $0.00 is refused (operator 2026-10-15).
15. People sheet: everyone with their share. Tapping a person shows the list from their side ("Viewing as Priya. Dots tick for them. Back to you"). "Switch account" re-picks who this phone is.
16. Payments sheet: a monochrome ring with round caps. The selected person's arc is black, the rest gray. Tapping a row selects that person. The ring draws in, one arc after another, when the sheet opens. "Paid" rows read "✓ Paid" in green.
17. History sheet: every change with an underlined Revert (or Restore) link and an animated undo arrow. The arrow swings back, then the row settles to a gray "✓ Reverted".

25. Pay sheet (operator, 2026-10-14): "Pay Sam" opens a sheet with the person's Items and their part of each, with no lines between Items (operator, 2026-10-15), then their part of tax, tip and discounts, and their total, all in regular weight. Below it sits the original pay pill: one black pill with the app mark and name, and a chevron end set off by a hairline that switches the app (a native select). Zelle shows a copy icon, copies the handle and says "Copied". The handles are listed in gray under the pill, then "I already paid". The pill and "I already paid" stay fixed at the bottom of the sheet. Marking paid closes the sheet, shows an undo bar, and turns the footer pill into a neutral outlined "✓ Paid" in the normal text color, with no green anywhere in the footer (operator, 2026-10-15). The footer keeps its items count line. Tapping the pill again shows "You paid $X" with "Mark as not paid". Screens `split-17` to `split-21`.
    The sheet has no hint sentence. A muted "What you had" label with the item count heads the Items, and "Your part of tax and tip" heads the charges, each over a hairline. An Item's description follows its name inline in muted gray (operator, 2026-10-16).

### Home

18. The operator chose the original start page over three variants (big receipt, viewfinder, one button).
19. Header: "SplitWith", a "Recent splits" link and the theme toggle. Intro: "Snap the receipt. Friends tap what they had and pay you back."
20. A large take-photo block: a paper drawing with a round black camera button, "Add the receipt", and "or choose a file". While reading it shows the stage ("Finding the text", "Matching prices", "Taking a few more seconds"). When ready it shows a card with the name, total, Item count and "Replace".
21. "How friends pay you": an app select and a handle field with no visible labels, and "+ Add another way to get paid".
22. "Who's at the table": one list of names, the first tagged "you", with an inline "+ Add your name" field. Enter adds the name and keeps the field open.
23. A footer with a reason line (red while blocked, for example "Add a receipt first.") and a "Create split link" black pill, gray while blocked.
26. Home is one sentence with recent splits below. Reading shows shimmering step words in the sentence, with no progress bar (operator, 2026-10-15). This wins over 18 to 23 where they conflict. The spec is `home-sentence/C3Recent.dc.html` (the empty home) and `home-sentence/R1Words.dc.html` (reading and ready).
    - The sentence is one 20 px muted paragraph: "I paid the bill. Add the receipt. Friends tap what they had and pay me back on Venmo at @username, or add an app. I'm your name, here with add friends." Actions (Add the receipt, Change, add an app, add friends, add more) are in the strong ink with an ink underline, and blanks have a gray one (operator, 2026-10-16). There is no intro line, no take-photo block and no section headings.
    - "Add the receipt" opens the same file chooser as before. Drop and paste still read a photo on desktop.
    - While reading, the step word takes the place of "Add the receipt" and follows the real stages: "Reading the receipt", "Finding the text", "Matching prices", then "Nearly done". Each new word rolls over like a counter: it rises 0.45 em into place over 280 ms ease-out while the word it replaced lifts 0.45 em and fades over 200 ms. Nothing loops while a read is held. Reduced motion only fades the new word in.
    - Once read, the clause is "I paid $X. Change." Change opens the chooser again.
    - Each added way to pay is an inline "or <app> at <handle>" clause. "add an app" at the end of the payment clause adds the next app and focuses its handle field, until every app is in. With two or more apps, a small plain × (the one names use) follows each app name and removes that app. Names follow "here with", and the first name is the Host. Friends can be named before the Host (operator, 2026-10-16).
    - Home does not scroll (operator, 2026-10-16). Only the footer is fixed, and nothing above it is pinned.
    - Recent splits lists this device's stored Splits under the sentence. Each row shows who, the day, how many people and the total. Home shows as many rows as fit above the footer. When some don't fit, a "See all N" link below the rows opens a bottom sheet that scrolls through every one. The section is gone when there are none.
    - Every page's header (home, the Split page and its loading and gone states, not-found) ends in the same three icons, 32 px apart, glyph centers at 292, 324 and 356 px on a 390 px phone (operator, 2026-10-17): an outline coffee cup named "Buy me a coffee" that opens the maker's Venmo (https://venmo.com/u/Jason-Zhang-162, new tab), the theme toggle, and Share. The cup draws at the same 14.7 px square as Share. Share sends the Split's link on a live Split and the site's link everywhere else; with no share sheet it copies the link and says "Link copied".
    - The footer matches the Split page: the reason line at the left, and a "Create link" pill at the right that is gray while blocked.

### First load

27. Loading screen, L1 "a receipt fills in" (operator, 2026-10-15). The spec is `loading/L1Receipt.dc.html`. While the bundle and Geist arrive, a 52 x 72 receipt outline with a torn bottom sits centered on the page, and three item lines and a thicker total line draw in, staggered 0, 120, 240 and 420 ms, on a 2.4 s loop with `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`. They wipe out at the end of each loop, and the receipt nudges down 3 px as each loop starts. It appears only after 300 ms, stays at least 300 ms once shown, and lifts when the first screen has mounted and Geist has loaded, or at 3 s in the fallback face. The page under it takes no taps, focus or screen-reader reads until then (`inert` and `aria-busy`). It fades out over 200 ms while the page fades in over 260 ms with `cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1)`. Under reduced motion the lines stand drawn and the receipt stays still. It is static HTML and CSS in `public/index.html` on every route. The Split page reuses that same element, never a copy, while its link connects (2026-10-17, operator: "show our load symbol" instead of the text "Opening split"). The app tells the page script it is busy or idle (`useBusyLoader` in `src/ui/document.ts`), and `data-boot="busy"` keeps the loader over a visible page with the same 300 ms delay and no 8 s fallback. A cold load still connecting when the app and Geist are ready goes from "wait" to "busy" with no fade, so the drawing carries on in place. The label "Opening split" stays as its accessible name. Each line slides through a clip rather than drawing with `stroke-dashoffset`, so it keeps moving while the bundle holds the main thread.

24. Keep the custom JS sheet on the web. Revisit `@expo/ui` BottomSheet for the native apps when they are prioritized.

### Connection

28. A bad connection is said out loud, never silently (operator, 2026-10-15). The Split page tracks one `Connection`: `online`, `slow` (the socket is open but an action has waited 3 s) or `offline` (the socket is closed or the browser reports no network).
    - Offline for more than 1.5 s shows "Offline. Reconnecting…" as a bare line in the header's free middle, between the wordmark and the icons, with no capsule behind it. The ellipsis is three dots that bounce in turn (each rises 3 px, 1200 ms a loop, 120 ms apart; under reduced motion they dim in turn instead), and a screen reader hears the sentence once. It stays muted, not red: red marks something the person can fix (decision 2), and a dropped connection is not one. It fades in over 240 ms and out over 180 ms (a 0.97 scale with it, fade only under reduced motion) and clears once the link has stayed up for 1 s, so a link that opens and drops in a loop does not blink it. A notice already showing never waits again to change, so slow goes straight to offline. There is no "Back online" line.
    - A slow action shows "Slow network. Saving…" in the same place as soon as it passes 3 s, and clears when the answer arrives.
    - The line sits in the header so it never covers the footer's pay pill or a sheet's fields, and a tall sheet stops below the header.
    - An action made while the socket is closed is refused at once and never queued. The browser's `navigator.onLine` only drives the notice; the socket decides whether a send goes out. A replay after a reconnect could land on a Split another phone changed meanwhile. A tap on a claim dot shows the bar "Couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again." and the dot stays as the server has it. A sheet shows the same words where it shows other save problems.
    - An action that gets no answer in 10 s fails with the same words, so no tap waits forever. The server may have applied it, so at 10 s the socket reconnects and the failure is delivered only after the server's join state has rendered. A claim, sheet edit or name pick that state shows landed reports no error and keeps its value.
    - Home's Create link says "Check your connection and try again." when the request never reaches the server, and a scan that read nothing while the browser is offline says "No connection, so the receipt couldn't be read. Try again once you're back online."
