Collapse motion (unchanged from round 7)

  • One spring on transform: translateY, tall to regular. Critically damped, no bounce, response 0.35 s (withSpring({ duration: 350, dampingRatio: 1 })).
  • Starts 80 ms after the tap so the filled check shows first. The list fades out with a 2 px blur over 120 ms. The chips fade in from scale 0.96 over 180 ms cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), 30 ms apart.
  • Interruptible. A drag picks the sheet up where it is and keeps the finger's velocity.
  • Reduced motion: a 200 ms cross-fade, no slide.

New motion

  • Closing the pick sheet with ✕ or a flick down: the same spring, down to the regular sheet in K.
  • Item and History sheets: rise on the same spring, scrim fades with progress. They close the way they came.
  • Split mode switch: the white segment slides under the labels, 200 ms ease-out. The rows below cross-fade over 150 ms. No height animation.
  • Check circle: the dot fades in from scale 0.9 over 150 ms ease-out. Unchecking has no motion.
  • Revert: the row dims to 45% over 150 ms and its button becomes "Reverted". The changed receipt value cross-fades with a 2 px blur.
  • Reduced motion: every sheet cross-fades over 200 ms. The segment jumps.
