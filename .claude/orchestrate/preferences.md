# Receipt Splitter standing orders

The operator's rulings that are in force today. One rule per line, numbered. Every agent brief points here, and these rules win over older plan text.
When a ruling changes, edit its line in place and add the date. Do not append a second, conflicting line. Superseded rulings and history are in `archive/preferences-2026-10-02.md`.

## Product: trust, money, limits

1. A Split is between friends and needs no verification. "That's me" lets any device take any name, and that device may mark the name paid. `payerOnly` guards honest mistakes, not security. (2026-10-01)
2. Two devices cannot hold the same name at once. The payer marks themselves paid. Participants can add people and remove anyone but the Host, not rename them; removing someone drops their claims (co-claimers absorb the Item, a sole claimer's Item goes unclaimed), their payment and their pick, and it is undoable from History. (2026-10-07, operator; was "not remove", 2026-10-01) B2 moves this to the People sheet: everyone is listed with their share, "+ Add" and Remove live there, and there is no sideways row of name chips, no fade and no long-press menu. (2026-10-14, operator chose B2)
3. Everyone in a Split can view and edit the receipt. Reverting someone else's change asks for confirmation. (2026-10-01)
4. Unclaimed Items are paid by nobody; they stay unclaimed. A leftover remainder in a "By amount" split counts as unclaimed. (2026-10-01)
5. A line shown as one row is claimed like any one-line Item, quantity or not: Who had it, Evenly, By shares and By amount apply to the whole line. After Split, each one-unit row is claimed the same way. (2026-10-03, replaces per-unit claims on a quantity line)
6. A receipt that does not add up, or reads oddly (e.g. a coupon worth more than its Item, a $0.00 subtotal), still produces a reading and can always be split: nothing about the receipt's numbers blocks creating the link or any operation. Home and the Split page show a quiet gray note instead. Structural validation only rejects what cannot be represented at all (no lines, unparseable amounts). (2026-10-05, operator: "even if it does not add up there should be an output"; was 2026-10-01)
7. No Turnstile. Creates are limited to 10 a minute and 50 a UTC day per IP, plus a global cap of 1,000 a UTC day. (2026-10-02, PR 34)
8. Production is app.splitwith.workers.dev on Cloudflare Workers Free: one Worker serves static assets, /api and /parties with Durable Objects. Every merge to main deploys. (2026-10-01)
9. Images only for receipts, no PDF. (2026-09-30)
10. Payment handles cannot change after create. There is no handle-edit control on the Split page. (2026-10-02)
11. "@jason-z" in mockups is a placeholder, not a real handle. Leave it. (2026-10-01)

## Product: receipt photo

12. Everyone in a Split can view the receipt photo. It is downscaled to about 150 KB, stored with the Split in its Durable Object, and deleted 14 days after create; the Split remains. The Host may upload at most twice. The photo may show whatever the receipt prints. (2026-10-01)
13. Home says nothing about who can see the photo. (2026-10-01)

(2026-10-02) On home there is no panel (B2): the footer with the reason line and Create sits in flow under the page, and a focused field is scrolled into view above the keyboard without the page jumping (2026-10-15, operator chose B2; was a rising bottom panel, 2026-10-04). ## Process

39. Owners commit locally and never push. The root verifies the exact SHA, pushes it once, opens the PR, and merges with a merge commit pinned by `--match-head-commit` on a clean verdict plus green CI. Never weaken a test to get green. The root does not edit product code. (2026-09-30)
40. Superseded 2026-10-03: the operator said "merge all PRs when done". The root merges each PR once it has a clean independent verdict and green CI, without waiting for a screenshot review in chat; screenshots still go to the operator after the merge. (was: PRs that change an interaction wait for the operator's review, 2026-10-01)
41. Verdicts are PASS, PASS+NOTES or FAIL. Clean means no FAIL. A reviewer gives FAIL only for a defect it reproduces that a user would see, that moves money or data wrongly, or that breaks a numbered rule; everything else is a note. Notes go to notes.md with a named fixer and are deleted in the PR that fixes them; a note never starts a fix round on its own. (2026-10-07, operator: fewer test-fix-retest loops; was 2026-09-30)
42. Budget per PR: about 30 minutes of test runs. Gates are lint, typecheck, unit, worker, e2e and size. Owners run unit, worker, lint, typecheck, size and the touched e2e specs on both engines locally; CI is the full e2e gate on both engines, so owners do not also run the full e2e suite locally (except the WebKit frame-timing pass of rule 45 when motion changed). Mutants only on core money logic, at most about 10. A fix round is checked on its delta: the gates plus the check that failed. (2026-10-07, operator; was 2026-10-01)
43. UI is proved by screenshots and the operator's review. Tests do not pin style values. (2026-10-01)
44. The real-iOS check in the iOS Simulator is the deciding gate for UI fixes: rerun the census tool in tools/flash/sim/census/tool/ on the fix head, and every flagged interaction must count 0. For keyboard-up and keyboard-down, count only the band above the keyboard (`keyboard-premise/above.sh`), judged against the static reference page `kbref.html`: frames whose signature also appears on the reference (iOS's own pan) do not count as defects. Use a 2-point touch_path, not tap. (2026-10-02; keyboard band 2026-10-03)
45. CI runs WebKit for the functional sheet and claim specs only; tests tagged @frame-timing skip on Linux. WebKit frame timing runs locally through motion.mjs on macOS before every UI PR. Chromium frame timing runs in CI. (2026-10-02)
46. A third fix to the same behavior stops for a premise check. (2026-09-30)
47. Size each PR so one owner stays under about 300k tokens; agents auto-compact at 400k (autoCompactWindow in the repo .claude/settings.json, so cloud sessions get it too, 2026-10-15). Model routing follows ~/.agents/pstack-models.md: sonnet is the default implementer (owners, fix rounds, delta audits) with opus as its advisor: the owner asks a short-lived opus agent before committing to a data shape, an architecture choice or a money-logic fix (the built-in advisor tool is off, operator 2026-10-09, because it cannot be scoped to subagents only); opus judges, reviews money/trust logic and takes the hardest tasks; haiku (Haiku 5.5) does read-only search, CI and deploy watching, status and log reads. (2026-10-09, operator; was opus owners, 2026-10-07)
48. All subagents run at medium effort. General-purpose briefs say so. (2026-10-01)
49. Subagents never file spawn_task chips; they list follow-ups in their report. (2026-10-02)
50. Post to the operator only on a merge, a deploy, a FAIL, a decision needed, or a blocker, or on /status. (2026-10-02)
51. At most 2 agents run full test suites at once, and only when `uptime` load is under 10. Kill only processes you started, by PID. (2026-10-02)
52. Never `cd x;` in shell commands; use `git -C` or `&&`. (2026-10-02)
53. Never commit or share the real receipt photo or its OCR. Tests use src/parse/fixtures/topgolf.tsv and e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png. (2026-10-01)
54. Test Splits on production: avoid creating them; if one is needed, use handle @test-debug and say so. (2026-10-02)

## Receipt reading (operator, 2026-10-03)

55. Sending receipt data to a server for reading is allowed now, including the photo and a third-party model API, reversing ADR 0001 for OCR (a new ADR records it). The API key lives in a Worker secret behind a same-origin /api route; scans get their own rate limit and daily cap. (2026-10-03)
56. The on-device OCR text is mostly fine; the failure is the parsed output, which is often completely wrong. Pick the reading path by best price to performance, OCR+LLM or VLM, decided by an eval on real receipts. (2026-10-03)
57. Receipt reading must be robust to model price rises and removals: prefer open-weight models served by several providers, keep the provider and model swappable by config, and keep a fallback that needs no paid model. (2026-10-03) Exception: the operator chose Google's Gemini for both passes for speed (2026-10-04); an ADR records it, and models and hosts stay swappable by config.
58. Quantities follow the receipt (supersedes 19): lines the receipt prints separately stay separate rows. A line printed with a quantity is one row with the quantity in its text, styled like the "6 items" count on a Bundle header (e.g. "3x"). It behaves as any one-line Item (rule 5). Its detail has a button that splits it into that many one-unit rows, which then behave as normal one-line Items. (2026-10-03)
59. Refreshing or reloading the home page resets it completely: no receipt, no photo, no names. Payment handles are kept across a reload, and a kept handle must work: if the field shows a handle, Create treats it as filled (fix the bug where a prefilled handle is treated as empty until the user types). (2026-10-03)

## Receipt reading design (operator approved 2026-10-03)
