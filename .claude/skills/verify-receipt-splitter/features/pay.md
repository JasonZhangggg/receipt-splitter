# Pay

The Pay sheet on the Split page `/s/<id>` (the footer's **Pay <Host>** pill opens it; the test id `breakdown` is the sheet) shows one wide pay pill, whatever the number of Payment handles the Host entered. A Split made with no handle shows no pill and no handles line, only **I already paid**. With one handle the pill reads **Pay Jason $12.05 on Venmo**. With two or more it reads **Pay Jason $12.05 on** and ends in the app's name and a chevron on a lighter capsule. That capsule is a native select named `Pay with`, listing the handles in the Host's order, so iOS shows its own picker and a desktop its own menu. The pill starts on the first handle. The device keeps the last pick for that Split in its `receipt-splitter:split:<id>` record (`payWith`), so a reload opens on it. A link pill keeps the full accessible name, such as `Pay Jason $12.05 on Venmo`, and opens the app's payment page with your Share to the cent. Zelle has no link. With Zelle picked, the pill shows a copy icon, is a button named `Copy jason@example.com to pay Jason $12.05 on Zelle`, copies the handle, and reads **Copied** with a check for about 1.6 s. Nothing copies the amount. A muted line under the pill lists every handle, Zelle's too, such as `Venmo @jason-z · Cash App $jasonz · Zelle jason@example.com`, and a long line breaks after a dot, never inside a handle. The breakdown's list keeps the same height for any number of handles, apart from that line wrapping. **I already paid** records a Payment of your Share. Once paid, the button reads **I haven't paid** and clears it. Your own amount reads **Your share** and anyone else's reads **Priya's share**, on the phone and the desktop, folded or open. Its share row's button names that person, such as "Priya's share $0.00, Hide Priya's breakdown". Under the label one gray line (`share-note`) says the paid state once there is one: "Paid $22.00", "Paid $22.00 · $8.00 too much" after the Share fell, "Paid $22.00 · $4.00 still owed" after it grew (with pills for the rest and **I paid the rest**), and for the Host on their own share "1 of 2 paid you" once anyone has paid. With no payment state there is no line. The line sits inside the share row's height, so the folded sheet never changes height for it. `e2e/share-note.spec.ts` drives every state. History lists Payments with no **Revert**.

Only the device that picked a name can record or clear that person's Payment. The server refuses anything else with `payerOnly`, and refuses an `undo` of a Payment change with `paymentNotRevertible`.

## Sub-features

- `pay-pills` shows the one pay pill, the `Pay with` picker that switches its app, the link carrying the Share to the cent, and the handles line.
- `pay-zelle` copies the Zelle handle from the pill when Zelle is picked.
- `pay-mark` records and clears your own Payment with **I already paid** and **I haven't paid**.
- `pay-status` shows the paid line under everyone else's share with no buttons, live.
- `pay-difference` shows "Paid $X · $Y too much" or "still owed" in every browser after a Share moves.
- `pay-refused` refuses a Payment for a name from a device that did not pick it.
- `pay-history` lists Payments in History without **Revert**.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Pick your name, check a line, and tap the footer's **Pay <Host>** pill or its "Your share" amount.
- Tap the underlined **People** link, then a person, to see their share in the footer and "Viewing as <name>" under the links.
- Tap the **History** link.
- Tap the **Payments** link to see who has paid.

## Driving it

Preconditions are the same as [claim.md](./claim.md). Create the Split over `POST /api/splits` with several handles, for example `"paymentHandles":[{"service":"venmo","handle":"jason-z"},{"service":"cashApp","handle":"jasonz"},{"service":"paypal","handle":"jasonzhang"},{"service":"zelle","handle":"jason@example.com"}]`. `e2e/pay.spec.ts` is the reference.

- **Pills (`pay-pills`).** Inside the test id `breakdown`, the pill is a link named `Pay <Host> $<amount> on <App>` whose `href` is the payment URL from `src/core/pay-links.ts` and whose `target` is `_blank`. Venmo on the web is `https://venmo.com/<user>?txn=pay&amount=14.20&note=Receipt%20split`. With two or more handles, switch the app with `getByRole("combobox", { name: "Pay with" }).selectOption("cashApp")`.
- **Zelle (`pay-zelle`).** Pick `zelle` in `Pay with` (or make it the only handle). The button `Copy <handle> to pay <Host> $<amount> on Zelle` is named "Copied" for about 1.6 s. Grant `clipboard-read` and `clipboard-write` to read the clipboard back.
- **Mark (`pay-mark`).** Tap **I already paid** inside `breakdown`. The sheet closes, an undo bar reads "Marked $X as paid" with **Undo** for 4 s, and the footer pill turns into a green **✓ Paid**. Reopening the sheet shows "You paid $X" with **Mark as not paid**. The test id `share` is the sheet's total and the footer's `owe` is the share.
- **Status (`pay-status`).** In another browser, open **People** and tap the payer. The footer's second line (`share-note`) reads `Paid $X`, and the sheet shows their Items with no links and no pay bar.
- **Difference (`pay-difference`).** After the payer marks paid, change a Claim from another browser so the Share moves. Both browsers show "Paid $X · $Y too much" under the share.
- **Refused (`pay-refused`).** From a page of another device, open `ws://<host>/parties/split-server/<id>` and send `{"ref":"forged","op":{"type":"recordPayment","by":"<id>","participantId":"<id>","device":"not-the-payer","amount":2200}}`. The answer is `{"type":"rejected","ref":"forged","rejection":{"reason":"payerOnly"}}`, and the payer's share shows no paid line.
- **History (`pay-history`).** Tap the **History** link. The Payment row shows no **Revert**.
- **Payments ring (`pay-ring`).** A Playwright drive samples every frame after tapping **Payments**: no frame has two ink arcs or another person's part in the middle. Step the draw with `document.getAnimations().filter((a) => a.animationName === "ring-draw")`, pausing each and setting `currentTime`.

## Gotchas

- Device ids travel in every state frame, so a hand-made frame can present another phone's id. The check stops every other device in the app, and **That's me** already lets anyone take a name in the open. The cleanup PR owns this.
- `venmo://paycharge` is used only in the native app. The web, phones included, uses the https link.
- The note is always "Receipt split", because the Receipt keeps no merchant name and Venmo notes can be public. Cash App and PayPal.me take no note.
- **Payments (`pay-status`).** Tap the **Payments** link. It is a bottom sheet (`payments-sheet`) on the phone and a popover under **Menu** from 1024 px, titled "Payments". The People sheet rows carry no check or "!" and their names read plain, `<name>` or `<name>, you`. Under the title "Everyone pays <Host first name>." reads in ink, and under it a smaller gray line reads "N of M paid you" for the Host or "N of M paid" for everyone else (test id `payments-summary`). M counts people other than the Host who owe or paid anything, and N those paid in full or more (a short payer is not counted), so a person with a $0.00 share and no payment is not counted. Below it, when something is unclaimed, " · $X unclaimed". A monochrome ring (round caps, 8 px, 168 px across) shows the chosen person's part: their arc is ink and the others gray (`c.dot`), with "Your part" or "<first name>'s part" and "of $Total" in the middle. The ring starts on the viewer's own row and draws in as one clockwise sweep, each arc starting when the sweep reaches it, up to 420 ms in (`ring-draw`, 620 ms each), when the sheet opens; under reduced motion it shows whole at once (`document.getAnimations()` holds no `ring-draw`). The list (role `list`, name "Payments") has one row per person, the viewer's own first and the rest in chip order, a hairline above each. A 12 px dot, the name (with the person icon after the viewer's) and the share on the right. Under the name a gray line says "Not paid" or "Nothing to pay", a green check and "Paid" once paid in full, a red "Paid $22.00 · $4.00 still owed" or "Paid $22.00 · $8.00 too much" when the share moved. The Host is listed with everyone who has paid, after everyone who still owes: a filled check that is not a button, their Items and share, and "Paid the bill". Their row picks nothing, since the ring holds only what friends owe. A tap on a row (`aria-pressed`) picks that person: the ink moves to their arc and the middle shows their part. The sheet updates when another phone pays or claims, and closes if its viewer is removed. The words come from `paymentStatus` in `src/ui/pay/model.ts`; `e2e/pay.spec.ts` drives the live updates, `e2e/people.spec.ts` the removal, and `e2e/desktop.spec.ts` the popover.
