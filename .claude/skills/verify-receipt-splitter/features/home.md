# Home

The home page at `/` is where a Host does everything, with no navigation. The page is white. The wordmark **Receipt Splitter** sits at the top left and a small **Example** link at the top right. The hero fills the space above a bottom sheet with a blank slip, a black camera button on it, **Add receipt**, and "Take or upload a photo". The sheet holds the payment row (an app picker showing **Venmo**, a handle field with the placeholder `@username`, and a **+** button), the name chips **Me** and **+ Add**, and a disabled **Create split link**. From 900 px the hero is a gray drop area with **Drop a receipt** and "Choose a photo, or paste one", left of a 380 px panel that holds the same controls. RS-7 wires the page to the draft: the hero, **Example**, the drop area, and the editor are in [Scan](./scan.md). The payment row and the name chips edit the draft, and **Create split link** enables once the Total check passes and a handle is filled in. RS-9 gives the action its handler.

## Sub-features

- `home-hero` shows the blank slip, its camera button, **Add receipt**, the subline, and the muted line "JPEG, PNG, or WebP" inside a dashed rounded box, or the drop area with **Drop a receipt** and a dashed border from 900 px. The dashes use the `dash` token, `#8A8E95` in light and `#6B6E75` in dark, at least 3:1 against the page and the gray fill. The formats line names the formats both Chrome and Safari decode. The page enforces no size cap. The whole space above the sheet is one Button. It takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring around the slip.
- `home-sheet` shows the bottom sheet under the scrolling page. It is 247 px tall at 390 by 844 when empty, taller than the 223 px round 6 prototype, and grows by the bottom safe-area inset. At 375 by 667 the camera button and **Add receipt** sit fully above it. **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"` and the blocked look. The handle field's text is 16 px. Below 360 px the picker shows only the glyph and the chevron, so `@username` fits the field at 320 px. From 900 px the sheet becomes a 380 px panel at the right, level with the top of the drop area. When less than 700 px is left between the safe-area insets, the slip draws at 72 percent and the upload box tightens, so the whole box stays above the sheet at 375 by 667, with or without both insets.
- `home-payment` is the payment row. The app picker is a native `select` named **Payment app** over the glyph, name, and chevron, with Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, and Zelle in that order. A row offers only its own app and the apps no other row uses. Each app shows its own placeholder: `@username`, `$cashtag`, `paypal.me/name`, or "Email or phone". **+** adds a row for the next unused app, and each added row has an x button named "Remove <app>". The rows and handles live in the draft.
- `home-names` shows **Me** as text and each added name as a chip with an x button named "Remove <name>". **+ Add** opens a **Name** field. Enter or leaving the field adds the trimmed name, and a blank name, **Me**, or a repeat is skipped.
- `home-sheet-drag` drags the phone sheet on its grab handle, a button named "Hide payment and names" or "Show payment and names" with `aria-expanded`. The sheet follows the finger and settles with a spring that starts from the release velocity, capped at 3,000 px/s, and lands where that velocity projects. A drag down collapses it to the action. It never leaves the page. A tap, Enter, or Space toggles it, and a touch during the settle catches it where it is drawn. Under reduced motion it settles at once. Its content scrolls on its own past 45 percent of the screen with `overscroll-behavior: contain`, and the action stays on screen.
- `home-press` is the press feedback of every Button. It follows react-native-web's `Pressable`, so ordinary browser press behavior applies in edge cases. A mouse, pen, or touch on a Button outside the scroll view presses at once. A touch on the hero or on **Example**, which sit in the scroll view, presses after 150 ms, so a scroll that starts on them does not flash a press. Enter and Space activate the focused Button. The press activates on release. A disabled Button draws no press and does not activate, and a Button that turns disabled during a press drops its press at once and stays at rest after it turns enabled again. The hero's slip scales to 0.97 over 120 ms. Every other Button scales to 0.96 over 100 ms. Both ease out over 200 ms on release. Under reduced motion a press dims to 0.7 with no scale. The payment app picker is a native `select`, not a Button, so it draws no press. Keyboard focus rings its inner face.
- `home-layout` keeps the header and the sheet content clear of the side safe-area insets, keeps the page from scrolling sideways from 320 px, and lays out the drop area and the panel from 900 px.
- `home-theme` follows the system light or dark setting with the Appendix E tokens. The dark page is `#121315` and the camera button and the enabled action are `#F2F2F3`. `theme-color` is `#FFFFFF` in light and `#121315` in dark. Plus Jakarta Sans 400 and 500 load as same-origin WOFF2 files from `/fonts/`, preloaded and cached as immutable, and size-matched local Arial faces stand in until they arrive, so no line or glyph box moves when they do.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the site's root URL on a phone or a desktop browser.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

- **Home, phone.** Run `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --expect-heading "Receipt Splitter" --expect-text "Add receipt" --expect-text "Venmo" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home.png --aria <out>/home.aria.txt`. The JSON line has `"ok":true`. The ARIA snapshot lists `button "Add receipt Take or upload a photo"`, `combobox "Payment app"`, `textbox "Venmo handle"`, `button "Add another payment app"`, `group "People at the table"`, `button "Add a name"`, and a disabled `button "Create split link"`.
- **Width, color scheme, and reduced motion.** Run one drive per case, with `<w>` in 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280, `<scheme>` in `light` and `dark`, and `<motion>` in `no-preference` and `reduce`: `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width <w> --color-scheme <scheme> --reduced-motion <motion> --expect-heading "Receipt Splitter" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home-<w>-<scheme>-<motion>.png`. Each JSON line has `"ok":true`, and its `media` shows the width, scheme, and motion setting the page received. The screenshots show the bottom sheet below 900 px and the drop area with the panel from 900 px. A drive does not look for clipped text or page overflow. For those, run the matrix spec on a port with no launched server: `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "home matrix"`. It fails on clipped text, horizontal page overflow, a missing Plus Jakarta Sans face, or a console error at 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280 px. With `SHOTS=<out>` it saves `home-<width>-<scheme>-<motion>.png` for each case.
- **Hero and sheet.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "empty home|Add receipt takes|blocked look|disabled Create|sheet stands|camera button|safe-area|drop area|design tokens|hero fills|lines up|380 px panel|placeholder fits"` checks that the page at 390 px shows exactly the words listed above and no others, that **Add receipt** takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring, and that **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"`, the `#EEEFF1` blocked look, a 180 ms `ease` background transition with none under reduced motion, and no press, not even from a synthetic keydown. In light and dark at 390 px it checks the slip's fill and its four 5 px hairline lines, the sheet's hairline and shadow, the 36 by 5 px grab handle, the 28 px 400 hero title, the ink **Me** chip, the 16 px handle text, and `theme-color`. It checks that the sheet is taller than 223 px at 390 by 844, that the camera button and **Add receipt** end above the sheet at 375 by 667, that the hero fills the space from the header to the sheet, that a 47 px top inset moves the header and the hero down by 47 px, that a 34 px bottom inset lifts **Create split link** by 34 px, that 47 px side insets in landscape keep the header and the sheet content 20 px inside them, that **Example** lines up with the wordmark, and that `@username` fits its field at 320 px. The sheet is the 380 px panel at 900 px and not at 899 px. At 1280 by 820 in dark mode the drop area ends left of the panel and starts level with it, with a 1.5 px hairline and the upload glyph, the page is `#121315`, and the camera button is `#F2F2F3`.
- **Components.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "primary Button|file row"` bundles `Button` and `FileRow` with esbuild into a blank page. In light and dark it checks that an enabled primary Button is a 54 px pill in `--action` with its `--on-action` label and the reason line above it in the yellow highlight, linked by `aria-describedby`, and that a click activates it once. It checks that the file row shows a 40 by 52 px thumbnail, the file name, "1 item" or "5 items", and a 44 px **Replace receipt** button with the camera glyph.
- **Press.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/press.spec.ts` bundles `Button` into a blank page and checks that a mouse press shows the press and the release activates once, that a touch press shows the press, that Enter and Space each activate once, that a disabled Button neither presses nor activates, also when it turns disabled during a held Space, and that reduced motion dims to 0.7 with no scale.
- **Fonts.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "fallback|fonts load"` checks that both faces load from `/fonts/` with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` while `/` stays cross-origin isolated. It removes the Plus Jakarta Sans faces before the page loads at 320, 390, and 1280 px and checks that every text line keeps its position and glyph box. It checks that the fallback metrics sample exactly the lines the page renders at 390 and 1280 px, and that no sample line is wider in the fallback face than in Plus Jakarta Sans. `npm test -- src/ui` checks that each face URL in `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts` names a WOFF2 file in `public/fonts/` whose name carries the first 10 hex digits of its SHA-256, and that every text and shape pair of the tokens meets WCAG AA.
- **Fallback metrics.** After a copy or font change, run `node assets/fonts/fallback-metrics.mjs` with Node 22.18 or later, or 23.6 or later on the 23 line. It measures each WOFF2 face against local Arial for every line in `fallbackSamples` from `src/ui/home-copy.ts` and rewrites `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts` with the face URLs and the metrics. It renames a face in `public/fonts/` to `plus-jakarta-sans-<weight>-<hash>.woff2` when its content changed, so the immutable cache never serves a stale face. It exits 1 and writes nothing when any line would be wider in the fallback.
- **Toast.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "toast"` bundles `src/ui/components/toast.tsx` with esbuild into a blank page and checks that a reduced-motion change does not restart its 4 s timer.

## Gotchas

- **Create split link** does nothing when enabled until RS-9 gives it a handler.
- The draft survives reloads in one browser context. A fresh Playwright context starts on the empty page.
- The hero's accessible name is its title and its subline together, such as "Add receipt Take or upload a photo". Match it by its start.
- The fonts are two requests under `/fonts/`. A font file name changes with its content, which is what makes the immutable cache header safe.
- The Playwright config starts its own server on `PORT`, so never point the spec at a port where `launch` is serving.
- The fonts are Latin subsets. Text outside them falls back to the system face.
