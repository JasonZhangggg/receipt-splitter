# Home

The home page at `/` is where a Host does everything, with no navigation. It is one centered column (440 px wide below 1280 px, 480 px from 1280 px) at every width, on the B2 tokens (`docs/design/b2/`, decision 26 and `docs/design/b2/home-sentence/`). The header has the wordmark **SplitWith** at the left and the sun/moon theme button at the right. Under it is one 20 px sentence (`data-testid="sentence"`): "I paid the bill. **Add the receipt**. Friends tap what they had and pay me back on **Venmo** at [@username]. I’m [your name], here with **add friends**." Actions are underlined words and blanks are fields with a gray rule. Under the sentence sit **+ Another way to get paid** and, when this browser has stored Splits, **Recent splits**. A fixed footer has the reason line at the left and a black **Create link** pill at the right, gray while something blocks it. The page's own scroller scrolls; the footer never does. Reading is in [Scan](./scan.md).

## Sub-features

- `home-header` shows **SplitWith** (a level 1 heading) and the theme button, a 44 px box named **Switch to dark mode** while the page is light and **Switch to light mode** while dark. Its sun and moon trade places with a rotate and scale; the new theme spreads from the button's center as a 600 ms circle (View Transitions), instantly under reduced motion or without the API. There is no Recent splits link.
- `home-receipt` is the sentence's first clause. Empty: "I paid the bill. **Add the receipt**." where **Add the receipt** is one button that opens the file chooser. Drop and paste anywhere on the page read a photo while the clause is empty. Reading: a `role="status"` named **Reading the receipt** reads "I paid the bill. <step>." and the step word (`data-testid="reading-stage"`) follows the real stages: "Reading the receipt" until the OCR engine is loaded, "Finding the text", "Matching prices" once the server reading is waited on, then "Taking a few more seconds" past 4 s. Each word fades in with a 2 px blur over 320 ms and sweeps a text gradient every 1.8 s; reduced motion swaps plain text. There is no progress bar. Read: "I paid $X at <place>. **Change**.", where the place is the business name the server read (left out when it read none or the device reader answered) (`data-testid="receipt"`, **Change** is named **Change receipt** and opens the chooser again). There is no thumbnail, item count or photo viewer on home.
- `home-payment` is the "pay me back on **Venmo** at [@username]" clause. The app word is a native `select` named **Payment app** laid invisibly over the word (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle, offering its own app and the unused ones); the blank is named "<App> handle" with its placeholder and grows with its text. **+ Another way to get paid** (named **Add another way to get paid**) adds an "or <app> at <handle>" clause for the next unused app, with an x named "Remove <app>". Handles are optional (preferences 71) and are kept across a reload (preferences 59).
- `home-names` is the "I’m [your name], here with **add friends**." clause. Until the Host's name is in, the blank after "I’m" is the field, named **Your name**. Enter adds it and moves the field, now named **Name**, to the end of "here with", focused (preferences 35); Enter there adds the next name and keeps the field open. Each name is a group with an x named "Remove <name>"; the Host's group is named "<name>, you" (preferences 85). **add friends** (**add more** once a friend is in, named **Add friends**) opens the field, or focuses **Your name** while no Host is in. Escape closes the field, and leaving it adds what it holds once the press that moved focus has landed. A repeated name shows "That name is already here." in red under the sentence.
- `home-footer` is the reason line and the pill, in one row. The line says exactly what blocks Create ("Add a receipt first.", "Reading the receipt.", "Add your name first.", or the handle rule such as "Venmo usernames need at least 5 characters. This one has 4.") and stays muted until it is a problem: a bad handle, or a tap on the gray pill. Then it is red. A receipt note that blocks nothing ("Items are $0.50 short of the subtotal.") shows muted, and a failed create shows red. The pill is `--action` at full strength when a tap creates the Split and at 28 percent while blocked. A tap on the gray pill acts (preferences 34): with no receipt it focuses **Add the receipt**, with a bad handle it focuses that field, with no name it focuses **Your name**, and a receipt the Split cannot hold opens the file picker. It is outside the scroll view, so a touch presses it at once, to 0.97 over 160 ms.
- `home-keyboard` keeps a focused field above the keyboard with no panel (preferences 17). While the keyboard covers the bottom of the page, the scroller gets that much room under its content and scrolls the focused field into the visible part of the visual viewport, again when the keyboard settles and when a name pushes the field down. The window never scrolls. Rule 17 is proved here with an emulated keyboard (`simulateKeyboard` in `e2e/motion.ts`); the operator checks a real iPhone.
- `home-history` is **Recent splits**, a region under the sentence listing the Splits this browser created or opened. The Split page stores each in `localStorage` under `receipt-splitter:split:<id>` as `recent: { names, total, added, seen }`. Rows show only those fields: the names, "<day> · N people" and the total, every entry seen within 60 days, newest by `added` first, at most 50 stored. Each row is a link named "<names>, <day>, N people, <total>". The region is gone when there are no entries.
- `home-press` is the press feedback. A touch on a control in the scroll view presses after 150 ms so a scroll that starts on it does not flash; the footer pill and anything outside the scroll view press at once. Icon buttons press to 0.9 over 140 ms, links and the photo block to 0.97. Under reduced motion a press dims to 0.7 with no scale.
- `home-layout` keeps the header and the footer clear of the side and bottom safe-area insets and the page from scrolling sideways from 320 px.
- `home-theme` follows the system light or dark setting with the B2 tokens (`src/ui/colors.ts`). The light page is `#FCFCFC` and the dark page is `#111111`. `theme-color` follows. Geist 400 and 500 load as same-origin WOFF2 files from `/fonts/`, preloaded and cached as immutable, and size-matched local Arial faces stand in until they arrive, so no line or glyph box moves when they do. The faces and their preloads are in `public/index.html`, so the fonts download beside the bundle.
- `first-load` is the receipt loader on every route (decision 27 in `docs/design/b2/README.md`). It lives in `public/index.html`, needs no bundle or font, and keeps the page hidden until the app has mounted its first screen (`announceMounted` in `src/ui/document.ts`) and Geist has loaded, or 3 s have passed. It shows only after 300 ms and stays at least 300 ms once shown. While it is up, `#root` is `inert` and `aria-busy`; without JavaScript neither is set and nothing is hidden. If the app never mounts, the page shows and takes input at 8 s, and a mount after that changes nothing on screen. Prove it with `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/boot.spec.ts`, which delays, fails and hangs the `/fonts/` requests with route interception. A plain drive cannot show it, because the local fonts arrive too fast.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the site's root URL on a phone or a desktop browser.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

- **Home, phone.** Run `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width 390 --height 844 --expect-heading "SplitWith" --expect-text "Add the receipt" --expect-text "Create link" --shot <out>/home.png --aria <out>/home.aria.txt`. The JSON line has `"ok":true`. The ARIA snapshot lists `button "Switch to dark mode"`, `button "Add the receipt"`, `combobox "Payment app"`, `textbox "Venmo handle"`, `textbox "Your name"`, `button "Add friends"`, `button "Add another way to get paid"`, and `button "Create link"`.
- **Width, color scheme, and reduced motion.** Run one drive per case, with `<w>` in 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280, `<scheme>` in `light` and `dark`, and `<motion>` in `no-preference` and `reduce`: `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width <w> --color-scheme <scheme> --reduced-motion <motion> --expect-heading "SplitWith" --expect-text "Create link" --shot <out>/home-<w>-<scheme>-<motion>.png`. Each JSON line has `"ok":true`. For clipped text and page overflow, run the matrix spec on a port with no launched server: `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "home matrix"`.
- **Read a receipt and create (lane 1).** A drive cannot choose a file, so run a Playwright script: open `/` at 390 by 844 on the Pixel 7 profile, click **Add the receipt**, choose `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` through the file chooser, wait for the `receipt` test id (the real on-device reading takes 10 to 60 s), fill **Venmo handle** with `jason-z`, type two names into **Your name** with Enter after each, then click **Create link** and catch the `POST /api/splits` body. The Items in `split.receipt.items` must match the same steps at trunk, and the page reaches `/s/<id>` with "Link ready.".
- **Keyboard (lane 2).** Install `iosKeyboard` from `e2e/motion.ts` as an init script (WebKit is the engine of record; CI runs it), tap **Venmo handle**, a second handle, **Your name**, and keep adding names. After each, `getBoundingClientRect()` of the field must sit inside `visualViewport` (`offsetTop` to `offsetTop + height`).
- **Blocked (lane 3).** With a read receipt, type `test` in **Venmo handle**: the reason line reads "Venmo usernames need at least 5 characters. This one has 4." in red and a tap on the gray pill focuses the handle. Clear it: the line reads "Add your name first." and a tap focuses **Your name**.
- **Desktop (lane 4).** At 1280 by 800 on the Desktop Chrome profile, the sentence and the footer share one left edge and width, and a dropped or pasted image reads (`e2e/scan.spec.ts` "an image dropped on the page and an image pasted").
- **Load a receipt in a spec.** `openExample(page)` in `e2e/files.ts` routes `/ocr-worker.mjs` to a worker that answers at once and `/api/scan` to a canned reply for SRD receipt 1040 (five Items, Tax $1.61, Service fees $3.45, Total $28.04), reloads, and chooses `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` through the page's own file chooser. There is no Example button.
- **Home spec.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts` covers the sentence on the empty page, the header and Recent splits, focus rings, blocked taps, the keyboard cases, safe-area insets, the centered column, names, icons, the select, the fonts and the fallback faces.
- **Components.** `-g "primary Button"` bundles `Button` with esbuild into a blank page and checks that an enabled primary Button presses with its reason line above it.
- **Press.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/press.spec.ts` bundles `Button` into a blank page and checks the press rules of a Button.
- **Fallback metrics.** After a copy or font change, run `FALLBACK_LOCAL="Liberation Sans" node assets/fonts/fallback-metrics.mjs` on Linux (the default `Arial` is on a Mac) with Node 22.18 or later. It measures each WOFF2 face against the local face for every line in `fallbackSamples` from `src/ui/home-copy.ts` and rewrites `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts`. It exits 1 and writes nothing when any line would be wider in the fallback.
- **Appearance.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/theme.spec.ts` flips home both ways on light and dark devices through the toggle and checks the page background, both `theme-color` tags, the stored key, the 600 ms reveal circle starting at the button's center, that a reload is already dark before the bundle runs, and that a Split page opened after a pick uses it.
- **Recent splits.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/history.spec.ts` creates a Split and checks that it heads the fresh home's list, and seeds stored Splits to check the list's place under the sentence, the order past five, the 1440 px column, the 60-day rule, an expired link dropping its entry, home and a Split page rendering with blocked storage, and the handle field above the iPhone keyboard.

## Gotchas

- A reload of home keeps only the payment handles, in one browser context. The receipt, the photo, and the names start empty. A fresh Playwright context also starts with no handle.
- The receipt button's accessible name is "Add the receipt". The sentence's words break across lines, so read it with `innerText` and collapse the whitespace; inline buttons start their own text runs.
- The name field stays open after Enter, so a spec that adds names types into the field it already holds; `addNames` in `e2e/files.ts` does that.
- The Playwright config starts its own server on `PORT`, so never point the spec at a port where `launch` is serving.
- The fonts are Latin subsets. Text outside them falls back to the system face.
