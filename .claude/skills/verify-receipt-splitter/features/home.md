# Home

The home page at `/` is where a Host does everything, with no navigation. The page is white. The wordmark **SplitWith** sits at the top left, and from 768 px a small **Example** link sits at the top right. The hero fills the space above a bottom sheet with a blank slip, a black camera button on it, **Add receipt** and "JPEG, PNG, or WebP". The sheet holds the payment row (an app picker showing **Venmo**, a handle field with the placeholder `@username`, and a **+** button), the name chip **+ Add name**, and a disabled **Create split link**. From 900 px the page is one centered column, 440 px wide below 1280 px and 480 px from 1280 px: a gray drop area with **Drop a receipt** and "Choose a photo, or paste one", then the payment row, the names, and **Create split link**. There is no sheet or side panel. RS-7 wires the page to the draft: the hero, **Example**, the drop area, and the receipt card are in [Scan](./scan.md). Home has no Item editor. Items are fixed on the Split page. The payment row and the name chips edit the draft, and **Create split link** enables once a read found Items and a name is added. A failed Total check blocks nothing. RS-9 gives the action its handler.

## Sub-features

- `home-hero` shows the blank slip, its camera button, **Add receipt** and the muted line "JPEG, PNG, or WebP" inside a dashed rounded box, or from 900 px the drop area with **Drop a receipt**, its subline, the formats line, and a dashed border, 380 px tall at 1280 px and up and 336 px below, with 32 px by 40 px of room around its content. The dashes use the `dash` token, `#8A8E95` in light and `#6B6E75` in dark, at least 3:1 against the page and the gray fill. The formats line names the formats both Chrome and Safari decode. The page enforces no size cap. The whole space above the sheet is one Button. It takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring around the slip.
- `home-sheet` shows the bottom sheet under the scrolling page. It is 277 px tall at 390 by 844 when empty, taller than the 223 px round 6 prototype, and grows by the bottom safe-area inset. At 375 by 667 the camera button and **Add receipt** sit fully above it. **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"` and the blocked look. The handle field's text is 16 px. Below 360 px the picker shows only the glyph and the chevron, so `@username` fits the field at 320 px. From 900 px there is no sheet. Its controls sit in the page column under the drop area. When less than 762 px is left between the safe-area insets, the slip draws at 66 percent and the upload box tightens, so the whole box stays above the sheet at 375 by 667, with or without both insets.
- `home-payment` is the payment row. The app picker is a native `select` named **Payment app** over the glyph, name, and chevron, with Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, and Zelle in that order. A row offers only its own app and the apps no other row uses. Each app shows its own placeholder: `@username`, `$cashtag`, `paypal.me/name`, or "Email or phone". **+** adds a row for the next unused app, and each added row has an x button named "Remove <app>". The rows and handles live in the draft.
- `home-names` shows each typed name as a chip with an x button named "Remove <name>" until the link exists, and none after. The first chip is the Host's and carries a small person icon named "you" after the name. The panel shows no section labels. The names group keeps the accessible name "People at the table" (preferences 84a). The payment row ends in an outlined + box (62 px wide, as tall as the field) named "Another way to get paid" that adds the next app. The first name is the Host's: **+ Add name** opens a **Your name** field, and while it is open the chip after it says **+ Add**. Enter adds the trimmed name and leaves a fresh **Name** field focused, so type, Enter, type, Enter adds names with no click. A click on **+ Add** while a field is open adds what it holds and keeps the field focused. Leaving the field adds the name and closes it once the press that moved focus has landed, so the sheet does not shift under the pointer. A blank name or a repeat is skipped. **Create split link** says "Add your name." until one is typed.
- `home-sheet-drag` is the phone panel's drag. The panel holds the payment rows, the names, and **Create split link** on one surface, and it never collapses. A drag that starts anywhere on the panel except a control (a field, a picker, a chip, or a button), such as the grab handle, the gutter, or the space beside the payment row, moves the whole panel with rubber-band resistance while the finger is down. On release it springs back to its rest position from the release velocity. A touch during the spring catches it where it is drawn. Under reduced motion a drag does not move it. A tap on a control, even one that wanders a few pixels, acts on the control and leaves the panel at rest. The panel and the hero take their size from `clientHeight`, which the iOS keyboard does not change, so the hero does not switch to compact for the keyboard. Only the panel moves for the keyboard (preferences 17); the page and its background stay still. Home clips the panel's fill (`overflow: clip`), so iOS has no room to scroll the page. On focus of a panel field the panel rises by the keyboard height it expects: the height the last open on this device settled at, kept in `localStorage` (never 0), else 330 px on iOS WebKit only (`navigator.standalone` and a coarse pointer). It rises over 350 ms on `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0.3, 0.1, 1)`, the iOS 27 keyboard's own curve. A warm keyboard is reported by the first `visualViewport` resize, and the rise starts in the next frame 20 ms plus that frame's wait into its curve. A cold keyboard is reported only at the end of its slide, so the rise starts on the first frame Safari draws at least 130 ms after focus (Safari draws nothing between focus and the keyboard's start). A warm keyboard first reports its height without the URL pill above it and adds the pill about 400 ms later; while a field has focus the panel only climbs, so neither report lowers it. Without a known height (Chromium device emulation, Android before its first keyboard) or after a focus that opened no keyboard, nothing moves until a resize reports one. On blur it falls in 90 ms. The toast (`data-follows-panel`) rides the same lift. **Create split link** sits 12 px above the keyboard unless that would push the panel's top off screen; the focused field always clears it. Panel fields take focus with `preventScroll`, since iOS otherwise scrolls the page to center a tapped field once the keyboard is up. `e2e/home.spec.ts -g "iPhone keyboard"` uses the shared iOS 27 keyboard model in `e2e/motion.ts`, which shrinks `visualViewport.height` by 336 px and pans it to reveal a field focused without `preventScroll`. The real check is the iOS Simulator census. Its content scrolls on its own past 45 percent of the page with `overscroll-behavior: contain`, and the action stays on screen while the keyboard is down.
- `home-create-blocked`: **Create split link** is white only when a tap creates the Split (preferences 34). Payment handles are optional (preferences 71), so a read Receipt and the Host's name are enough. Otherwise, in the read state, it is gray with one quiet line above it that says what is wrong, and a tap creates nothing and goes to the field: "Add your name." opens the name field, a handle its app would refuse focuses that row and says why (the first rule it breaks, such as "Venmo usernames need at least 5 characters. This one has 4." or "Cashtags need at least one letter."), and a Receipt the Split cannot hold opens the photo picker. Venmo and Cash App keep their username rules; PayPal and Zelle take any text up to 80 characters. The rules live in `HANDLE_RULES` in `src/core/operations.ts`, which the server applies too, and the words in `blockerText` in `src/ui/home-copy.ts`. Before a read, Create is off and says nothing.
- `home-history` is **Recent splits**, the Splits this browser created or opened. The Split page stores each one in `localStorage` under `receipt-splitter:split:<id>` as `recent: { names, total, added, seen }`, with no server call. Home lists them from the header **Menu**: its **History** row opens the list, and the row shows only when at least one entry is listed. Below 900 px a tap opens a bottom sheet with a scrim (`data-testid="recent-sheet"`); from 900 px a 360 px popover anchored under the **Menu** button, right edges aligned. Both are a dialog named **Recent splits** inside a React Native Web `Modal`, and list every entry seen within 60 days, newest by `added` first. The browser keeps at most 50. Each row is a link to `/s/<id>` named "<names>, <day>, <total>", such as "Jason, Sam, and 2 more, Oct 1, $9.90". It closes on a scrim tap or a swipe down (phone), a click outside (desktop), and Escape, and focus returns to the **Menu** button. The sheet uses the `BottomSheet` glide; the popover scales from 0.96 and fades in over 200 ms from its top-right corner and fades out over 150 ms, opacity only under reduced motion. An entry leaves 60 days after this browser last opened its Split, and at once when its link says the Split has expired. A row's press fills it with `sheetIn` after a 150 ms touch hold, at once for a mouse hover, and fades over 200 ms on release.
- `home-appearance` is the light and dark change, a row in the header **Menu** (a hamburger button named **Menu**, the rightmost header button on home at every width, 44 by 44, always shown, the same popover as the Split page's). The row is a `moon` named **Dark mode** while the page is light and a `sun` named **Light mode** while it is dark; **History** sits above it when there are stored Splits. Choosing the row flips the scheme for every page on this browser and stores `receipt-splitter:appearance` as `light` or `dark`; picking the scheme the device already uses removes the key, so the page follows `prefers-color-scheme` again. `public/index.html` is the export's HTML template. Its head script reads the key before the first paint, sets `data-theme` on `<html>`, and points both `theme-color` tags at the picked scheme's `field` color; its `rs-ground` style paints the page background from `data-theme` or the media query. The flip crossfades the page over 200 ms with a view transition, and swaps at once under reduced motion or without `document.startViewTransition`. Open **Menu** first, then click the row. A `drive --click 'role=button[name="Menu"]' --reduced-motion reduce` shows the flipped page settled; without reduced motion the screenshot can land mid-fade. The pick does not survive a drive, since each drive opens a fresh browser.
- `home-dropzone` on a phone is the dashed **Add receipt** box, which fills the page from the header to the panel at every height. From 900 px it keeps a 336 px (380 px from 1280 px) minimum in the centered column. Its dashes use the `dash` token, `#B0B4BA` in light and `#4A4D53` in dark, about 2:1 against the page. Nothing moves when the keyboard opens.
- `home-press` is the press feedback of every Button. It follows react-native-web's `Pressable`, so ordinary browser press behavior applies in edge cases. A mouse, pen, or touch on a Button outside the scroll view presses at once. A touch on the hero or on **Example**, which sit in the scroll view, presses after 150 ms, so a scroll that starts on them does not flash a press. Enter and Space activate the focused Button. The press activates on release. A disabled Button draws no press and does not activate, and a Button that turns disabled during a press drops its press at once and stays at rest after it turns enabled again. The hero's slip scales to 0.97 over 120 ms. Every other Button scales to 0.96 over 100 ms. Both ease out over 200 ms on release. Under reduced motion a press dims to 0.7 with no scale. The payment app picker is a native `select`, not a Button, so it draws no press. Keyboard focus rings its inner face.
- `home-layout` keeps the header and the sheet content clear of the side safe-area insets, keeps the page from scrolling sideways from 320 px, and lays out the centered column from 900 px.
- `home-theme` follows the system light or dark setting with the Appendix E tokens. The dark page is `#121315` and the camera button and the enabled action are `#F2F2F3`. `theme-color` is `#FFFFFF` in light and `#121315` in dark. Plus Jakarta Sans 400 and 500 load as same-origin WOFF2 files from `/fonts/`, preloaded and cached as immutable, and size-matched local Arial faces stand in until they arrive, so no line or glyph box moves when they do.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the site's root URL on a phone or a desktop browser.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

- **Home, phone.** Run `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --expect-heading "SplitWith" --expect-text "Add receipt" --expect-text "Venmo" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home.png --aria <out>/home.aria.txt`. The JSON line has `"ok":true`. The ARIA snapshot lists `button "Add receipt JPEG, PNG, or WebP"`, `combobox "Payment app"`, `textbox "Venmo handle"`, `button "Another way to get paid"`, `group "People at the table"`, `button "Add a name"`, and a disabled `button "Create split link"`.
- **Width, color scheme, and reduced motion.** Run one drive per case, with `<w>` in 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280, `<scheme>` in `light` and `dark`, and `<motion>` in `no-preference` and `reduce`: `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width <w> --color-scheme <scheme> --reduced-motion <motion> --expect-heading "SplitWith" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home-<w>-<scheme>-<motion>.png`. Each JSON line has `"ok":true`, and its `media` shows the width, scheme, and motion setting the page received. The screenshots show the bottom sheet below 900 px and the centered column from 900 px. A drive does not look for clipped text or page overflow. For those, run the matrix spec on a port with no launched server: `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "home matrix"`. It fails on clipped text, horizontal page overflow, a missing Plus Jakarta Sans face, or a console error at 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280 px. With `SHOTS=<out>` it saves `home-<width>-<scheme>-<motion>.png` for each case.
- **Hero and sheet.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "empty home|Add receipt takes|blocked look|disabled Create|sheet stands|camera button|safe-area|centered column|charcoal|design tokens|hero fills|lines up|page column|bottom sheet|placeholder fits"` checks that the page at 390 px shows exactly the words listed above and no others, that **Add receipt** takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring, and that **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"`, the `#EEEFF1` blocked look, a 180 ms `ease` background transition with none under reduced motion, and no press, not even from a synthetic keydown. In light and dark at 390 px it checks the slip's fill and its four 5 px hairline lines, the sheet's hairline and shadow, the 36 by 5 px grab handle, the 26 px 400 hero title, the muted **Add name** chip, the 16 px handle text, and `theme-color`. It checks that the sheet is taller than 223 px at 390 by 844, that the camera button and **Add receipt** end above the sheet at 375 by 667, that the hero fills the space from the header to the sheet, that a 47 px top inset moves the header and the hero down by 47 px, that a 34 px bottom inset lifts **Create split link** by 34 px, that 47 px side insets in landscape keep the header and the sheet content 22 px inside them, that **Example** lines up with the wordmark, and that `@username` fits its field at 320 px. At 1024 and 1440 px the drop area is centered and the payment row and **Create split link** share its left edge and width, with no sheet. The sheet holds the controls at 899 px and is gone at 900 px. At 1280 by 820 in dark mode the drop area shows the upload glyph, the page is `#121315`, and the camera button is `#F2F2F3`.
- **Icon cost.** After `npm run build`, run `module-cost.mjs "data:image/svg+xml;utf8"` from the worktree root. It finds the Metro module that holds the inlined SVG icons and prints its raw and brotli size and `brotliDelta`, the bytes it adds to the entry's brotli size. It reported a `brotliDelta` of 551 when this step was written. A module that grows this number is a bundle-size regression that `npm run size` shows only as a total.
- **Names and Example.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "names go in|Example shows"` types "Sam", Enter, "Priya", Enter after one click and checks two chips and a focused empty **Name** field, and that **Example** shows at 768 px and not at 390 px.
- **Components.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "primary Button|receipt card"` bundles `Button` and `ReceiptSummary` with esbuild into a blank page. In light and dark it checks that an enabled primary Button is a 54 px pill in `--action` with its `--on-action` label and the reason line above it in the yellow highlight, linked by `aria-describedby`, and that a click activates it once. It checks that the receipt card reads the total over "1 item" or "5 items", that only a card with a photo has **View receipt photo**, and that it and **Replace receipt** each call their handler once.
- **Press.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/press.spec.ts` bundles `Button` into a blank page and checks that a mouse press shows the press and the release activates once, that a touch press shows the press, that Enter and Space each activate once, that a disabled Button neither presses nor activates, also when it turns disabled during a held Space, and that reduced motion dims to 0.7 with no scale.
- **Fonts.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "fallback|fonts load"` checks that both faces load from `/fonts/` with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` while `/` stays cross-origin isolated. It removes the Plus Jakarta Sans faces before the page loads at 320, 390, and 1280 px and checks that every text line keeps its position and glyph box. It checks that the fallback metrics sample exactly the lines the page renders at 390 and 1280 px, and that no sample line is wider in the fallback face than in Plus Jakarta Sans. `npm test -- src/ui` checks that each face URL in `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts` names a WOFF2 file in `public/fonts/` whose name carries the first 10 hex digits of its SHA-256, and that every text and shape pair of the tokens meets WCAG AA.
- **Fallback metrics.** After a copy or font change, run `node assets/fonts/fallback-metrics.mjs` with Node 22.18 or later, or 23.6 or later on the 23 line. It measures each WOFF2 face against local Arial for every line in `fallbackSamples` from `src/ui/home-copy.ts` and rewrites `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts` with the face URLs and the metrics. It renames a face in `public/fonts/` to `plus-jakarta-sans-<weight>-<hash>.woff2` when its content changed, so the immutable cache never serves a stale face. It exits 1 and writes nothing when any line would be wider in the fallback.
- **Appearance.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/theme.spec.ts` flips home both ways on light and dark devices and checks the page background, both `theme-color` tags, and the stored key, that a reload is already dark when the HTML finishes parsing and before the bundle runs, that a Split page opened after a pick uses it, and that the menu button keeps its place when Recent splits appears.
- **Recent splits.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/history.spec.ts` creates a Split and checks that it heads the fresh home's list and opens its link, and seeds stored Splits to check: no History row on a fresh home; the menu button's place and size; every entry listed newest first past five; the phone sheet closing on a scrim tap and Escape with focus back on the menu button; the 1440 px popover under the icon closing on an outside click and Escape with focus back; the 60-day rule; an expired link dropping its entry and the History row hiding with the last one; the dashed box filling the page at 390 by 844 and 390 by 700; home and a live Split page rendering with blocked storage; and the handle field above the iPhone keyboard with the box unmoved and only the panel risen. `node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/history-shots.mjs <port> <out>` against a launched port saves the screenshot matrix at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark, with no entries, with the icon, with the list open, and at 390 px with the keyboard up.
- **Toast.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "toast"` bundles `src/ui/components/toast.tsx` with esbuild into a blank page and checks that a reduced-motion change does not restart its 4 s timer.

## Gotchas

- **Create split link** does nothing when enabled until RS-9 gives it a handler.
- A reload of home keeps only the payment handles, in one browser context. The receipt, the photo, and the names start empty. A fresh Playwright context also starts with no handle.
- The hero's accessible name is its title and its subline together, such as "Add receipt JPEG, PNG, or WebP". Match it by its start.
- The fonts are two requests under `/fonts/`. A font file name changes with its content, which is what makes the immutable cache header safe.
- The Playwright config starts its own server on `PORT`, so never point the spec at a port where `launch` is serving.
- The fonts are Latin subsets. Text outside them falls back to the system face.
