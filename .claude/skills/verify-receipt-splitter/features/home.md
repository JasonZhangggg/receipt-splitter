# Home

The home page at `/` is where a Host does everything, with no navigation. The page is white. The wordmark **Receipt Splitter** sits at the top left and a small **Example** link at the top right. The hero fills the space above a bottom sheet with a blank slip, a black camera button on it, **Add receipt**, and "Take or upload a photo". The sheet holds the payment row (an app picker showing **Venmo**, a handle field with the placeholder `@username`, and a **+** button), the name chips **Me** and **+ Add**, and a disabled **Create split link**. From 900 px the hero is a gray drop area with **Drop a receipt** and "Click to choose a photo, or paste one.", left of a 380 px panel that holds the same controls. RS-6 builds this page static. Its Buttons draw their press but do nothing yet.

## Sub-features

- `home-hero` shows the blank slip, its camera button, **Add receipt**, and the subline, or the drop area with **Drop a receipt** from 900 px. The whole space above the sheet is one Button. It takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring around the slip.
- `home-sheet` shows the bottom sheet under the scrolling page. It is 247 px tall at 390 by 844 when empty, taller than the 223 px round 6 prototype, and grows by the bottom safe-area inset. At 375 by 667 the camera button and **Add receipt** sit fully above it. **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"` and the blocked look. The handle field's text is 16 px. Below 360 px the picker shows only the glyph and the chevron, so `@username` fits the field at 320 px. From 900 px the sheet becomes a 380 px panel at the right, level with the top of the drop area.
- `home-press` is the press feedback of every Button. On the web the look follows react-native-web's `Pressable`. A mouse or pen presses at once, and so does a touch on a sheet Button. A touch on the hero or on **Example**, which sit in the scroll view, presses after 150 ms, or on release when a tap ends sooner, and never once the browser takes the touch for a scroll. A sheet Button lets go when the browser takes its touch for a drag. A key press draws the press exactly when the browser activates the Button with it. Space and Enter press until key-up, alone or with Shift. Space also presses with Control or Cmd. Enter with Alt, Control, or Cmd, Space with Alt, and every other key draw nothing, and none of them activates. Combinations follow the same rule: Enter presses only while none of Alt, Control, and Cmd is held, and Space presses whenever Alt is up, so Shift+Control+Space presses and Shift+Alt+Enter does not. Enter activates on key-down, and each auto-repeat key-down activates again. Space activates on key-up, so a held Space follows Alt: Alt pressed during the hold drops the press, and Alt released during the hold draws it again. A key tap or a mouse click shows no tap look after release. A held mouse or pen press lets go when that pointer leaves and presses again when it returns with its button still down. A pointer that returns with no button held, as after a release the page never saw, draws nothing. A mouse that returns with only a non-primary button held draws nothing either. A press activates only when it both starts and ends on the Button. A right-click, a middle-click, or a press with Alt or Control held draws no press, and a press with Shift or Cmd held presses and activates. A second finger, or a mouse passing over with or without its button down, never ends a held touch or a held Space press. While a touch is held, a second pointer on another Button draws no press there, but a mouse click on it still activates it. A mouse press on another Button ends a held pen press. The payment app picker draws its press on its inner face, the glyph, the name, and the chevron. **Me** is chip text, not a Button. A disabled Button draws no press from any input, a synthetic keydown included. A Button that turns disabled during a press, or disables itself when it activates, rests at once and stays at rest after the release. A press that starts while the Button is disabled draws nothing and does not activate, even when the Button turns enabled before the release. The payment app picker draws its focus ring around the same inner face it presses. A release, a context menu, a blur, a scroll, a text selection, or a cancelled touch ends a press. The hero's slip scales to 0.97 over 120 ms. Every other Button scales to 0.96 over 100 ms. Both ease out over 200 ms on release. Under reduced motion a press dims to 0.7 with no scale.
- `home-layout` keeps the header and the sheet content clear of the side safe-area insets, keeps the page from scrolling sideways from 320 px, and lays out the drop area and the panel from 900 px.
- `home-theme` follows the system light or dark setting with the Appendix E tokens. The dark page is `#121315` and the camera button and the enabled action are `#F2F2F3`. `theme-color` is `#FFFFFF` in light and `#121315` in dark. Plus Jakarta Sans 400 and 500 load as same-origin WOFF2 files from `/fonts/`, preloaded and cached as immutable, and size-matched local Arial faces stand in until they arrive, so no line or glyph box moves when they do.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the site's root URL on a phone or a desktop browser.

Its touch cases run at 390 by 500, where the page scrolls by about 100 px, and its mouse cases at 1280 px, by 340 px tall where a case needs the page to scroll. The timing bounds are these. - **Fallback metrics.** After a copy or font change, run `node assets/fonts/fallback-metrics.mjs` with Node 22.18 or later, or 23.6 or later on the 23 line. It renames a face in `public/fonts/` to `plus-jakarta-sans-<weight>-<hash>.woff2` when its content changed, so the immutable cache never serves a stale face. It exits 1 and writes nothing when any line would be wider in the fallback.
- **Toast.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "toast"` bundles `src/ui/components/toast.tsx` with esbuild into a blank page and checks that a reduced-motion change does not restart its 4 s timer.

## Gotchas

- Every Button on the page is static in RS-6. A press shows, and nothing happens after it. RS-7 wires the hero, **Example**, the payment row, and the name chips. RS-9 wires **Create split link**.
- The hero's accessible name is its title and its subline together, such as "Add receipt Take or upload a photo". Match it by its start.
- The fonts are two requests under `/fonts/`. A font file name changes with its content, which is what makes the immutable cache header safe.
- The Playwright config starts its own server on `PORT`, so never point the spec at a port where `launch` is serving.
- The fonts are Latin subsets. Text outside them falls back to the system face.
