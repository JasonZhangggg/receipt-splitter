# Home

The home page at `/` is where a Host does everything, with no navigation. The page is white. The wordmark **Receipt Splitter** sits at the top left and a small **Example** link at the top right. The hero fills the space above a bottom sheet with a blank slip, a black camera button on it, **Add receipt**, and "Take or upload a photo". The sheet holds the payment row (an app picker showing **Venmo**, a handle field with the placeholder `@username`, and a **+** button), the name chips **Me** and **+ Add**, and a disabled **Create split link**. From 900 px the hero is a gray drop area with **Drop a receipt** and "Click to choose a photo, or paste one.", left of a 380 px panel that holds the same controls. RS-6 builds this page static. Its Buttons draw their press but do nothing yet.

## Sub-features

- `home-hero` shows the blank slip, its camera button, **Add receipt**, and the subline, or the drop area with **Drop a receipt** from 900 px. The whole space above the sheet is one Button. It takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring around the slip.
- `home-sheet` shows the bottom sheet under the scrolling page. It is 248 px tall at 390 by 844 when empty, taller than the 223 px round 6 prototype, and grows by the bottom safe-area inset. At 375 by 667 the camera button and **Add receipt** sit fully above it. **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"` and the blocked look. The handle field's text is 16 px. From 900 px the sheet becomes a 380 px panel at the right, level with the top of the drop area.
- `home-press` is the press feedback of every Button. On the web the look follows react-native-web's `Pressable`. A mouse or pen presses at once, and so does a touch on a sheet Button. A touch on the hero, which sits in the scroll view, presses after 150 ms, or on release when a tap ends sooner, and never once the browser takes the touch for a scroll. A sheet Button lets go when the browser takes its touch for a drag. Space and Enter press until key-up, other keys and Cmd+Enter draw nothing, and a key tap or a mouse click shows no tap look after release. A held mouse or pen press lets go when that pointer leaves and presses again when it returns, and it activates only when it both starts and ends on the Button. A right-click, a middle-click, or a press with Alt or Control held draws no press. A second finger, or a mouse passing over with or without its button down, never ends a held touch or a held Space press. A release, a context menu, a blur, a scroll, a text selection, or a cancelled touch ends a press. The hero's slip scales to 0.97 over 120 ms. Every other Button scales to 0.96 over 100 ms. Both ease out over 200 ms on release. Under reduced motion a press dims to 0.7 with no scale.
- `home-layout` keeps the header and the sheet content clear of the side safe-area insets, keeps the page from scrolling sideways from 320 px, and lays out the drop area and the panel from 900 px.
- `home-theme` follows the system light or dark setting with the Appendix E tokens. The dark page is `#121315` and the camera button and the enabled action are `#F2F2F3`. `theme-color` is `#FFFFFF` in light and `#121315` in dark. Plus Jakarta Sans 400 and 500 ship inline as WOFF2 data URLs, and size-matched local Arial faces stand in until they decode, so no line or glyph box moves when they arrive.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the site's root URL on a phone or a desktop browser.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

- **Home, phone.** Run `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --expect-heading "Receipt Splitter" --expect-text "Add receipt" --expect-text "Venmo" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home.png --aria <out>/home.aria.txt`. The JSON line has `"ok":true`. The ARIA snapshot lists `button "Add receipt Take or upload a photo"`, `button "Payment app, Venmo"`, `textbox "Venmo handle"`, `button "Add another payment app"`, `group "People at the table"`, `button "Add a name"`, and a disabled `button "Create split link"`.
- **Width, color scheme, and reduced motion.** Run one drive per case, with `<w>` in 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280, `<scheme>` in `light` and `dark`, and `<motion>` in `no-preference` and `reduce`: `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width <w> --color-scheme <scheme> --reduced-motion <motion> --expect-heading "Receipt Splitter" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home-<w>-<scheme>-<motion>.png`. Each JSON line has `"ok":true`, and its `media` shows the width, scheme, and motion setting the page received. The screenshots show the bottom sheet below 900 px and the drop area with the panel from 900 px. A drive does not look for clipped text or page overflow. For those, run the matrix spec on a port with no launched server: `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "home matrix"`. It fails on clipped text, horizontal page overflow, a missing Plus Jakarta Sans face, or a console error at 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280 px. With `SHOTS=<out>` it saves `home-<width>-<scheme>-<motion>.png` for each case.
- **Hero and sheet.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "empty home|Add receipt takes|blocked look|sheet stands|camera button|safe-area|drop area"` checks that the page at 390 px shows exactly the words listed above and no others, that **Add receipt** takes Tab focus with a 2 px ring, that **Create split link** has `aria-disabled="true"`, the `#EEEFF1` blocked look, a 180 ms `ease` background transition with none under reduced motion, and no press. It checks that the sheet is taller than 223 px at 390 by 844, that the camera button and **Add receipt** end above the sheet at 375 by 667, that a 34 px bottom safe-area inset lifts **Create split link** by 34 px, and that 47 px side insets in landscape keep the header and the sheet content 20 px inside them. At 1280 by 820 in dark mode it checks that the drop area ends left of the 380 px panel and starts level with it, that the page is `#121315`, and that the camera button is `#F2F2F3`.
- **Press.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "press|tap|touch|mouse|right-click|scroll|key|finger|pen|Space|focus|wheel|select|Alt|Control|middle|cancel|drag|Enter"` runs every press rule in `home-press`. A sheet Button is **Add a name**, outside the scroll view, at 390 by 844. The hero sits in the scroll view. Its touch cases run at 390 by 500, where the page scrolls by about 100 px, and its mouse cases at 1280 px, by 340 px tall where a case needs the page to scroll. The timing bounds are these. A press shows within 50 ms of touch-down, mouse-down, or pen-down. A held touch on the hero shows its first pressed frame 150 to 260 ms after touch-down. After touch taps of 60, 100, and 140 ms on the hero, the last pressed frame lands 70 to 180 ms after release under reduced motion and 270 to 380 ms after it with motion, which adds the 200 ms press-out. After a 40 ms Space tap or a 60 ms mouse click on the hero, or a 60 ms tap on a sheet Button, the last pressed frame lands less than 70 ms after release under reduced motion and less than 270 ms after it with motion.
- **Fonts.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "fallback"` removes the Plus Jakarta Sans faces before the page loads at 320, 390, and 1280 px and checks that every text line keeps its position and glyph box. It checks that the fallback metrics sample exactly the lines the page renders at 390 and 1280 px, and that no sample line is wider in the fallback face than in Plus Jakarta Sans. `npm test -- src/ui` checks that `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts` holds exactly the bytes of the two WOFF2 files in `assets/fonts/`, and that every text and shape pair of the tokens meets WCAG AA.
- **Fallback metrics.** After a copy or font change, run `node assets/fonts/fallback-metrics.mjs` with Node 22.18 or later, or 23.6 or later on the 23 line. It measures each WOFF2 face against local Arial for every line in `fallbackSamples` from `src/ui/home-copy.ts` and rewrites `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts` with the faces and the metrics. It exits 1 and writes nothing when any line would be wider in the fallback.
- **Toast.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "toast"` bundles `src/ui/components/toast.tsx` with esbuild into a blank page and checks that a reduced-motion change does not restart its 4 s timer.

## Gotchas

- Every Button on the page is static in RS-6. A press shows, and nothing happens after it. RS-7 wires the hero, **Example**, the payment row, and the name chips. RS-9 wires **Create split link**.
- The hero's accessible name is its title and its subline together, such as "Add receipt Take or upload a photo". Match it by its start.
- Metro has no WOFF2 asset type, so the fonts are not separate requests. Do not look for font files in the network log. They are inside the entry bundle.
- The Playwright config starts its own server on `PORT`, so never point the spec at a port where `launch` is serving.
- The fonts are Latin subsets. Text outside them falls back to the system face.
