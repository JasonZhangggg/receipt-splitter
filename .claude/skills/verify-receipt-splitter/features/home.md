# Home

The home page at `/` is where a Host does everything, with no navigation. It is one centered column (440 px wide below 1280 px, 480 px from 1280 px) at every width, on the B2 tokens (`docs/design/b2/`). The header has the wordmark **SplitWith** at the left, a **Recent splits** text link that shows only when this browser has stored Splits, and the sun/moon theme button at the right. There is no menu, share or Example button. Under it the intro "Snap the receipt. Friends tap what they had and pay you back.", then the receipt block (a thin-ruled card with a paper drawing and a round black camera button, **Add the receipt**, and the underlined **or choose a file**), **How friends pay you** (an app select and a handle field, no labels, and **+ Add another way to get paid**), **Who’s at the table** (one list of names and **+ Add your name**), and a fixed footer with a reason line and a black **Create split link** pill, gray while something blocks it. The page's own scroller scrolls; the footer never does. The receipt block is in [Scan](./scan.md).

## Sub-features

- `home-header` shows **SplitWith** (a level 1 heading), **Recent splits** (only with stored Splits, preferences 37) and the theme button, a 44 px box named **Switch to dark mode** while the page is light and **Switch to light mode** while dark. Its sun and moon trade places with a rotate and scale; the new theme spreads from the button's center as a 600 ms circle (View Transitions), instantly under reduced motion or without the API.
- `home-receipt` is the block under the intro. Empty: the paper drawing with the camera button and **Add the receipt** (one button), plus **or choose a file**; from 900 px a muted "Drop or paste a photo here" sits between them, and the card's rule darkens while a photo is dragged over the page. Reading: the card shows a slip thumbnail, **Reading the receipt**, the stage line ("Finding the text", then "Matching prices" once the server reading is waited on, then "Taking a few more seconds" when it passes 4 s) and a 2 px bar. Read: the card shows the thumbnail (a button named **View receipt photo** while the page holds the photo), **Receipt**, the total, "N items" and the underlined **Replace** (named **Replace receipt**). The title is not the merchant (plan Appendix B).
- `home-payment` is **How friends pay you**, a region. Each row is a native `select` named **Payment app** (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Zelle, in that order, offering its own app and the unused ones) and a handle field named "<App> handle" with its placeholder. **+ Add another way to get paid** adds a row for the next unused app, and each added row has an x button named "Remove <app>". Handles are optional (preferences 71) and are kept across a reload (preferences 59). The rules under the fields are a thin line that darkens on focus; no ring is drawn on them.
- `home-names` is **Who’s at the table**, a region. Each name is a group with a dot, the name, a remove x named "Remove <name>", and for the first name (the Host) a "you" tag; the group's accessible name is "<name>, you" for the Host (preferences 85). **+ Add your name** (**+ Add name** after the first) swaps for an inline field named **Your name** (**Name** after the first). Enter adds the trimmed name and keeps the field open and focused for the next (preferences 35), Escape closes it, and leaving it adds what it holds and closes it once the press that moved focus has landed. A repeated name shows "That name is already here." in red.
- `home-footer` is the reason line and the pill. The line says exactly what blocks Create ("Add a receipt first.", "Reading the receipt.", "Add your name first.", or the handle rule such as "Venmo usernames need at least 5 characters. This one has 4.") and stays muted until it is a problem: a bad handle, or a tap on the gray pill. Then it is red. A receipt note that blocks nothing ("Items are $0.50 short of the subtotal.") shows muted, and a failed create shows red. The pill is `--action` at full strength when a tap creates the Split and at 35 percent while blocked. A tap on the gray pill acts (preferences 34): with no receipt it focuses **Add the receipt**, with a bad handle it focuses that field, with no name it opens the **Your name** field, and a receipt the Split cannot hold opens the file picker. It is outside the scroll view, so a touch presses it at once, to 0.97 over 160 ms.
- `home-keyboard` keeps a focused field above the keyboard with no panel (preferences 17, 33). While the keyboard covers the bottom of the page, the scroller gets that much room under its content and scrolls the focused field into the visible part of the visual viewport, again when the keyboard settles and when a name pushes the field down. The window never scrolls. Rule 17 is proved here with an emulated keyboard (`simulateKeyboard` in `e2e/motion.ts`); the operator checks a real iPhone.
- `home-history` is **Recent splits**, the Splits this browser created or opened. The Split page stores each in `localStorage` under `receipt-splitter:split:<id>` as `recent: { names, total, added, seen }`. The header link opens the list: below 900 px a bottom sheet with a scrim (`data-testid="recent-sheet"`), from 900 px a 360 px popover under the link, right edges aligned. Both are a dialog named **Recent splits** and list every entry seen within 60 days, newest by `added` first, at most 50 stored. Each row is a link named "<names>, <day>, <total>". It closes on a scrim tap, a click outside or Escape, and focus returns to the link. The link hides when the last entry is gone.
- `home-press` is the press feedback. A touch on a control in the scroll view presses after 150 ms so a scroll that starts on it does not flash; the footer pill and anything outside the scroll view press at once. Icon buttons press to 0.9 over 140 ms, links and the photo block to 0.97. Under reduced motion a press dims to 0.7 with no scale.
- `home-layout` keeps the header and the footer clear of the side and bottom safe-area insets and the page from scrolling sideways from 320 px.
- `home-theme` follows the system light or dark setting with the B2 tokens (`src/ui/colors.ts`). The light page is `#FCFCFC` and the dark page is `#111111`. `theme-color` follows. Geist 400 and 500 load as same-origin WOFF2 files from `/fonts/`, preloaded and cached as immutable, and size-matched local Arial faces stand in until they arrive, so no line or glyph box moves when they do.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the site's root URL on a phone or a desktop browser.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

- **Home, phone.** Run `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width 390 --height 844 --expect-heading "SplitWith" --expect-text "Add the receipt" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home.png --aria <out>/home.aria.txt`. The JSON line has `"ok":true`. The ARIA snapshot lists `button "Switch to dark mode"`, `button "Add the receipt"`, `combobox "Payment app"`, `textbox "Venmo handle"`, `button "Add another way to get paid"`, `region "Who’s at the table"` with `button "Add your name"`, and `button "Create split link"`.
- **Width, color scheme, and reduced motion.** Run one drive per case, with `<w>` in 320, 375, 390, 900, 1024, and 1280, `<scheme>` in `light` and `dark`, and `<motion>` in `no-preference` and `reduce`: `verify.mjs drive --port 8811 --path / --width <w> --color-scheme <scheme> --reduced-motion <motion> --expect-heading "SplitWith" --expect-text "Create split link" --shot <out>/home-<w>-<scheme>-<motion>.png`. Each JSON line has `"ok":true`. For clipped text and page overflow, run the matrix spec on a port with no launched server: `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts -g "home matrix"`.
- **Read a receipt and create (lane 1).** A drive cannot choose a file, so run a Playwright script: open `/` at 390 by 844 on the Pixel 7 profile, click **Add the receipt**, choose `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` through the file chooser, wait for the `receipt` test id (the real on-device reading takes 10 to 60 s), fill **Venmo handle** with `jason-z`, add two names with **Add your name** and Enter, then click **Create split link** and catch the `POST /api/splits` body. The Items in `split.receipt.items` must match the same steps at trunk, and the page reaches `/s/<id>` with "Link ready.".
- **Keyboard (lane 2).** Install `iosKeyboard` from `e2e/motion.ts` as an init script (WebKit is the engine of record; CI runs it), tap **Venmo handle**, a second handle, **Add your name**, and keep adding names. After each, `getBoundingClientRect()` of the field must sit inside `visualViewport` (`offsetTop` to `offsetTop + height`).
- **Blocked (lane 3).** With a read receipt, type `test` in **Venmo handle**: the reason line reads "Venmo usernames need at least 5 characters. This one has 4." in red and a tap on the gray pill focuses the handle. Clear it: the line reads "Add your name first." and a tap opens **Your name**.
- **Desktop (lane 4).** At 1280 by 800 on the Desktop Chrome profile, the receipt block, both sections and the pill share one left edge and width, a drag over the page darkens the card's rule, and a dropped or pasted image reads (`e2e/scan.spec.ts` "an image dropped on the page and an image pasted").
- **Load a receipt in a spec.** `openExample(page)` in `e2e/files.ts` routes `/ocr-worker.mjs` to a worker that answers at once and `/api/scan` to a canned reply for SRD receipt 1040 (five Items, Tax $1.61, Service fees $3.45, Total $28.04), reloads, and chooses `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` through the page's own file chooser. There is no Example button.
- **Home spec.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/home.spec.ts` covers the words on the empty page, the header, focus rings, blocked taps, the keyboard cases, safe-area insets, the centered column, names, icons, the select, the fonts and the fallback faces.
- **Components.** `-g "primary Button|receipt card"` bundles `Button` and `ReceiptSummary` with esbuild into a blank page and checks that an enabled primary Button presses with its reason line above it, and that the receipt card reads "Receipt", the total, "N items" and **Replace**, that only a card with a photo has **View receipt photo**, and that each calls its handler once.
- **Press.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/press.spec.ts` bundles `Button` into a blank page and checks the press rules of a Button.
- **Fallback metrics.** After a copy or font change, run `FALLBACK_LOCAL="Liberation Sans" node assets/fonts/fallback-metrics.mjs` on Linux (the default `Arial` is on a Mac) with Node 22.18 or later. It measures each WOFF2 face against the local face for every line in `fallbackSamples` from `src/ui/home-copy.ts` and rewrites `src/ui/fonts.generated.ts`. It exits 1 and writes nothing when any line would be wider in the fallback.
- **Appearance.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/theme.spec.ts` flips home both ways on light and dark devices through the toggle and checks the page background, both `theme-color` tags, the stored key, the 600 ms reveal circle starting at the button's center, that a reload is already dark before the bundle runs, and that a Split page opened after a pick uses it.
- **Recent splits.** `PORT=8912 npx playwright test e2e/history.spec.ts` creates a Split and checks that it heads the fresh home's list, and seeds stored Splits to check the link's place, the order past five, the phone sheet and the 1440 px popover closing with focus back on the link, the 60-day rule, an expired link dropping its entry, home and a Split page rendering with blocked storage, and the handle field above the iPhone keyboard.

## Gotchas

- A reload of home keeps only the payment handles, in one browser context. The receipt, the photo, and the names start empty. A fresh Playwright context also starts with no handle.
- The receipt button's accessible name is "Add the receipt". On desktop the text above **or choose a file** is outside it.
- The name field stays open after Enter, so a spec that adds names types into the field it already holds; `addNames` in `e2e/files.ts` does that.
- The Playwright config starts its own server on `PORT`, so never point the spec at a port where `launch` is serving.
- The fonts are Latin subsets. Text outside them falls back to the system face.
