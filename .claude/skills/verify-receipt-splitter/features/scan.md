# Scan

The Host reads a Receipt on the home page `/`. Items are fixed on the Split page by tapping a line, and home has no Item editor. A tap on the hero opens the photo picker. On a desktop, an image dropped anywhere on the page or pasted from the clipboard is read too, and a file that is not an image gets the one line "That file is not a photo." Drop and paste read a photo only on the empty page. On the read state a dropped file changes nothing, and a Host replaces the Receipt with the camera button on the receipt card. While OCR runs, the slip turns into a skeleton with seven lines that darken one at a time over a progress bar, under the title **Reading receipt**. The read state shows the compact receipt card: the thumbnail, the file name, the Item count, the total, and **Replace receipt**. Below 900 px the card stands centered above the sheet with a 116 by 148 px thumbnail. From 900 px it is one row in the page column. A tap on the thumbnail opens the photo viewer. A read the Split would refuse is repaired when it lands, so Create still sends it and the Split page fixes the rows: an Item with a price and no name goes out as "Item 6" (its place among the read rows, blank $0.00 rows included), a Bundle with no name as "Bundle 2" (its place among the Bundles), a price or Adjustment over $100,000 goes out as $0.00, a Subtotal below $0.00 or over the limit as the Items' sum with a price over the limit counted at the limit, and a Total below $0.00 or over the limit as the Subtotal plus the Adjustments. Both sums are kept between $0.00 and the limit. A read with no Items keeps **Create split link** off with "Couldn't read any items. Try another photo.", because a Split holds at least one Item. An on-device read whose total line the OCR cannot read still gives its Items, and the Items and charges stand in for the total, with the quiet gray note "Couldn't read the total, so check the rows." A coupon worth more than its Item takes that Item to $0.00 with its own coupon and sends only the excess as one discount on the whole receipt. More whole-receipt discounts than a Split holds merge into one "Discounts" row, and a negative tip line is dropped. A read with more than 100 Items, or more than 19 taxes, fees and discounts (the Tip takes the 20th Adjustment), keeps it off with "A split holds up to 100 items. This receipt has 120." or "A split holds up to 19 taxes, fees and discounts. This receipt has 25." Otherwise it is gray with one short reason until the Host has a name; a Payment handle is optional. A failed Total check blocks nothing and shows a quiet note instead. A reload of home starts over and keeps only the Payment handles. RS-9 gives the enabled action its handler.

## Sub-features

- `scan-input` opens the picker with `accept="image/*"` from the hero, reads a dropped or pasted image, and refuses any other file with one line in the Toast. Photos decode with `imageOrientation: "from-image"`, so an EXIF-rotated phone photo reads upright.
- `scan-ocr` is the on-device OCR in `public/ocr-worker.mjs`. It finds text on the photo scaled to 960 px on its long side. When that text is under 19 px tall, or the photo is more than twice as tall as it is wide, it looks again at up to 1280 px on the short side and 8 million pixels, in 1280-row strips that overlap by 160 rows, so a long receipt keeps readable text and memory stays near a normal scan's. When tall boxes cover more than twice the area of wide ones, the photo lies sideways, and text is found again on the photo turned a quarter clockwise. When the mean confidence of a reading is under 0.7, eight boxes are read again flipped, and the photo is read upside down if those read more surely. Boxes keep the photo's own pixels with their corners along the text, so `groupRows` in `src/parse/rows.ts` first turns them by the quarter turn most of them share. `bench/run-ocr.mjs` measures it.
- `scan-reading` shows the skeleton slip, **Reading receipt**, and a progress bar that fills over the model download, then OCR. With motion on, each line reaches full ink 150 ms after the one above it, in a 1.6 s loop. Under reduced motion the lines hold still, the bar has no transition, and `document.getAnimations()` is empty. The state ends when the Items show.
- `scan-card` is the compact card. The Example has no photo, so its card shows the receipt glyph and has no **View receipt photo**. A reload keeps the card but not the photo, which lives only in the page's memory.
- `scan-viewer` opens the photo the Host scanned in a dialog named for the file. Below 900 px it fills the screen on a dark ground with **Close photo**, the file name, and **Replace receipt** across the top. From 900 px it is a 540 by 640 px frame over the scrim with **Close photo** beside it. Escape, a click on the scrim, and **Close photo** close it. It fades and scales in from 0.96 over 240 ms and out over 180 ms, and under reduced motion it only fades. It lives in `src/ui/photo-viewer/` for V2-6b.
- `scan-repair` is `sendable` in `src/draft.ts`. It runs once per read and gives the Receipt Create sends, or null when no Item was read. An Item a discount took below zero is $0.00, like an OCR price over `MAX_CENTS`. The Total check note then names the gap, and the Split page fixes the row.
- `scan-tip` reads the tip from the receipt's tip lines as one Tip Adjustment, $0.00 when the receipt prints none, so the Split page can set it.
- `scan-reason` puts one reason above a gray **Create split link** in the read state: "Couldn't read any items. Try another photo.", one of the two limit reasons above, a handle reason, or "Add your name.", as `home-create-blocked` in [home.md](./home.md) describes. A tap on the gray button creates nothing and goes to the field; for a Receipt reason it opens the photo picker. The empty and reading states show no reason and the button is off.
- `scan-mismatch` puts a muted note with no highlight above an enabled **Create split link** while the Total check fails: "Items are $0.50 short of the subtotal.", "Items are $0.50 over the subtotal.", or "Tax and charges are $0.61 short of the total."
- `scan-draft` keeps only the Payment handles in local storage under `receipt-splitter:draft` (version 2). A reload of home clears the Receipt, the photo, and the names, and keeps the handles (preferences 59). Replacing the Receipt keeps the handles and names.
- `scan-offline` keeps a scan working offline once the model is cached. `public/sw.js` answers from Cache Storage only while the browser is offline, and the page asks it to keep `/`, the entry, the fonts, and the worker script. `sw.js` keeps only same-origin files on its own allowlist, never `/api`, `/parties`, or `/s/`. The OCR worker keeps the ORT JavaScript files with the runtime and the models.
- `scan-bundles` reads a group of Items under a $0.00 header as one Bundle when a later discount's label echoes the header's name, prints a percent, and that percent of the Items right under the header equals the discount, as Topgolf's "Beer Bucket Discount (10%)" does. The Bundle holds only those Items. A discount with no percent, or whose percent matches no run of Items under the header, stays on the whole receipt. The header is the Bundle's name and not an Item, and the discount is a `bundleDiscount` Adjustment that names the Items. A read Item with no name and no price is dropped, and leaves its Bundle. A Bundle left with no Item keeps its discount on the whole receipt. A $0.00 header that no discount closes stays an Item. The created Split holds the Bundles and is stored at `schemaVersion` 2. A Split without a Bundle is stored at version 1.
- `scan-example` fills the draft with SRD receipt 1040 as its checked label reads, named "Example receipt", and keeps the Payment handles.
- `scan-server` reads the photo on home through `scan-route` (V2-7b, `src/scan/client.ts`). The page runs the on-device OCR first, crops the photo to the OCR boxes with a margin of two box heights, downscales it to at most 2048 px on its long edge, and encodes it as a JPEG through a canvas, so it carries no EXIF. It lowers the quality from 0.85 in steps until the body fits 1 MiB, then posts it with the OCR rows as plain lines. The reply maps to a Receipt: an Item's detail lines join with ", ", each discount printed under it adds "$2.00 off" to that detail, a Bundle becomes a `bundleDiscount` Adjustment, and a fee or deposit becomes a service charge. An Item whose printed discounts take it below $0.00, such as a $4.00 coupon on a $1.00 Item, keeps its price before discounts and sends the rest of its line as a discount on the whole receipt. Any status but 200, a network failure, a reply without the reply's shape, or no reply in 15 s falls back to the on-device reading of the same OCR, with no message. That includes the route's 400 for OCR text over 8,000 characters or a photo over 2,560 px, and its 429 when the day's budget is spent. **Reading receipt** stays until either reading lands, and the bar fills to 80% over OCR and holds there while the server reads. The page marks `scan-picked` and `scan-read` with `performance.mark`.
- `scan-detail` shows an Item's detail under its name on the Split page, in gray, up to two lines. An Item with no detail falls back to splitting a size printed on the name line. The item sheet has a **Detail** field under the name field in one grouped surface, with the placeholder "Add detail (optional)". Its name and Detail fields show the row's two lines, so "Michelob Ultra 16oz Aluminum Bottle" opens as "Michelob Ultra" over "16oz Aluminum Bottle". The first edit to either field saves that size as the detail, so the row keeps both lines. Anyone can edit it. History lists the edit ("Added "Large" to Fries", "Fries Large → Small", "Removed "Small" from Fries"), and Revert restores it. A Split with any detail is stored at `schemaVersion` 3.
- `scan-route` is `POST /api/scan`, which reads a receipt on the server (ADR 0008). Home calls it from V2-7b. The body is JSON, `{ "photo": "data:image/jpeg;base64,...", "ocrLines": ["..."] }`, with an `origin` header equal to the site's own origin. It answers 403 `{ "refused": "origin" }` for any other origin or none, 413 `bodyTooLarge` over 1 MiB, 400 `rejected` for a photo that is not a JPEG data URL, a JPEG with no frame header or whose longer side is over `SCAN_MAX_IMAGE_PX` (2,560), or a bad body, 503 `unavailable` without the `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` secret, 429 `rateLimited` or `dailyLimit` with `retry-after`, 502 `unreadable` when no pass gives a usable reading, and 200 with the reading: `items` (name, price after the discounts printed under it, quantity, details, discounts), `bundles` (name, Item indexes, discounts), `adjustments`, `subtotal`, `total`, and `check` (`passed`, `gaps`, and `problems` when it fails). A 200 carries `x-scan-cost`, the dollars counted against the budget, and `x-scan-passes` only when the `SCAN_DEBUG_HEADERS` var is `true`, as in a lane's `.dev.vars`. The shipped `wrangler.jsonc` sets it to `false`.
- `scan-limits` allows one address 6 scans a minute and 20 a UTC day, and all addresses 2,000 a UTC day, apart from the create limits, and refuses with 429 `dailyLimit` once the day's spend plus one worst-case scan would pass the budget. The values are the `SCAN_PER_MINUTE`, `SCAN_PER_DAY`, `SCAN_DAILY_CAP`, `SCAN_DAILY_BUDGET_USD`, and `SCAN_WORST_CASE_USD` vars in `wrangler.jsonc`.
- `scan-passes` reads with pass 1 on `SCAN_PASS1_FLEX_HOSTS` and aborts that call at `SCAN_PASS1_FLEX_DEADLINE_MS` (3.5 s). When it misses, fails, or comes back empty, pass 1 tries each host in `SCAN_PASS1_STANDARD_HOSTS` in turn. Pass 2 runs on `SCAN_PASS2_HOSTS` only when the total fails, and the penny review on `SCAN_PENNY_HOSTS` only when the total passes off by 1 to 3 cents. Either review must answer within `SCAN_REVIEW_BY_MS` of the scan's start (ADR 0010). Each scan logs one `"event":"scan"` JSON line with its `path` (the pass-1 tier, the review and how it ended, and the edits refused for naming a total) and each call's step, host, time, tokens, cost, and outcome, and never the photo, the OCR lines, or a reply's text.
- `scan-harness` is `bench/scan/run.mjs`, which posts each fixture in `bench/scan/fixtures/` to a running route and prints one table scored by `bench/scan/score.mjs`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `/` on a phone, tap **Add receipt**, and take or choose a photo.
- Open `/` at 900 px or wider, and click **Drop a receipt**, drop an image on the page, or paste one.
- Tap **Example** at the top right of the empty page, at 768 px or wider.
- In the read state, tap the camera button on the card to replace the Receipt, or the thumbnail to see the photo.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

`verify.mjs drive` loads one route and cannot choose a file, so every flow past the empty page runs through `e2e/scan.spec.ts`. Stop the lane's `launch` first, because the spec starts its own server on `PORT`.

- **Read SRD receipt 1040.** `PORT=8812 npx playwright test e2e/scan.spec.ts -g "chosen through the picker"` picks `bench/data/srd-1040.jpg`, sees **Reading receipt**, then the card with "srd-1040.jpg", "5 items", and "$28.04", no Item list, the five Items in the stored draft, no reason, and **Create split link** on. A tap on it focuses the Venmo handle, and a handle then brings up "Add your name."
- **Failing Total check.** `-g "fails the Total check"` reads `e2e/fixtures/srd-1040-no-sprite.jpg`, whose Sprite line is painted out, sees "4 items", the muted note "Items are $2.50 short of the subtotal.", and creates the link.
- **Viewer.** `-g "photo viewer"` opens the photo from the thumbnail at 390 px and closes it with **Close photo** and Escape, closes it with a scrim click at 1280 px, replaces the photo with the card's camera, and checks the Example card has nothing to open.
- **Fix it on the Split page.** `-g "price over the limit"` reads `e2e/fixtures/example-price-over-limit.png`, whose Perrier prints $1,000,003.50 and whose Total prints $1,000,028.04. Home shows "5 items", "$28.04", no Item list, and the note "Items are $3.50 short of the subtotal." Create opens the Split page with Perrier at $0.00, and a price of 3.50 there clears the note.
- **Nothing read.** `-g "no Items keeps"` reads `e2e/fixtures/blank.png` and gets "0 items", no editor, and "Couldn't read any items. Try another photo."
- **Desktop input.** `-g "1280 px"` refuses a dropped PDF with one line, then reads a dropped image and a pasted image.
- **Server reading, mocked.** `-g "reading through the server"` answers `/api/scan` with `page.route`: an invented reply whose discount lands as "$2.00 off" on the Split page, 429 and 502 that fall back to the on-device reading, a request held past 15 s that falls back, and a large noisy photo whose upload stays under 1 MiB at 2048 px. `CPU_THROTTLE=4` runs the cases under a 4x CPU throttle.
- **Server reading, live.** Write `.dev.vars` and `launch` as in the route section below, then choose a photo in a Playwright script. The request to `/api/scan` returns 200 and the card shows the route's reading. To see the fallback, block the route in that script with `await page.route("**/api/scan", (route) => route.abort())`, or answer it with any status but 200. Without `.dev.vars` the route answers 503 and every scan reads on the device, which is how the rest of `e2e/scan.spec.ts` runs.
- **Topgolf Bundles.** Choose `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` through the picker in a Playwright script against a `launch`ed port. The card reads "topgolf-synthetic.png / 9 items / $207.29". The Receipt Create sends holds nine Items (Gameplay, then two runs of Michelob Ultra, Guinness, High Noon Pineapple, and High Noon Watermelon, with Guinness ×3 in the first run and Guinness ×2 and Watermelon ×2 in the second) and two "Beer Bucket Discount (10%)" rows at -4.51 and -4.63, with no mismatch note. No "Beer Bucket" Item is stored. The image is drawn from `src/parse/fixtures/topgolf.tsv` by `e2e/fixtures/make-fixtures.py` and holds no payment lines. Never use the real Topgolf photo in a committed test.
- **EXIF.** `-g "EXIF"` reads `e2e/fixtures/srd-1040-exif-rotated.jpg`, whose pixels lie sideways with EXIF orientation 6, as the same five Items in the stored draft.
- **Reading state.** `-g "reading state"` holds the dictionary download for 3 s, checks that the seven lines darken in order, and that reduced motion runs no animation.
- **Reload and offline.** `-g "reload keeps the draft"` scans, edits, waits for the service worker, goes offline, reloads, and scans again. `-g "payment apps and names"` switches to Cash App, adds Venmo with **+**, adds two names, removes one, and reloads.
- **Sheet.** `-g "the sheet"` drags the grab handle a short way and watches the panel spring back, catches it during the spring, and fills it until its content scrolls. In `e2e/home.spec.ts`, `-g "the phone panel|no way to pay|no name|says why"` drags it from beside the payment row, checks that the action moves with it with no gap, taps the handle field with a small wander, creates a Split with no handle, and taps the gray **Create split link** with no name and with a 4-letter Venmo username.
- **Screens.** For a screenshot of a state, run the spec with `--trace on` and save a frame, or use a short Playwright script against a `launch`ed port, as `/tmp/swarm-RS-7/owner/shots.mjs` does.

### The scan route and its harness

Preconditions:

- `.dev.vars` in the worktree root holds the key. Write it without printing it: `node -e 'const fs=require("fs");const k=fs.readFileSync(process.env.HOME+"/.config/receipt-eval/env","utf8").match(/OPENROUTER_API_KEY=(\S+)/)[1];fs.writeFileSync(".dev.vars","OPENROUTER_API_KEY="+k+"\nSCAN_DEBUG_HEADERS=true\n",{mode:0o600})'`. `SCAN_DEBUG_HEADERS=true` makes the route send `x-scan-cost` and `x-scan-passes`, which `run.mjs` reads. It is `false` in `wrangler.jsonc`, so production never sends them. `.dev.vars` is gitignored, and `.dev.vars.example` names the key with an empty value. Never commit, print, or log the key.
- `verify.mjs launch --port 9211` reported `"ok":true` after `.dev.vars` was written. Wrangler reads `.dev.vars` only at start.
- Live scans cost money. Check the balance before and after a run with `node ~/receipts-eval/credits.mjs`.

- **Score the fixtures.** `node bench/scan/run.mjs --url http://localhost:9211 --runs 3` prints the table and exits 1 below `bench/scan/fixtures/floor.json`. t1 is the Topgolf receipt drawn from `src/parse/fixtures/topgolf.tsv`, and its row should show both Bundles right.
- **Score the operator's receipts.** `node bench/scan/run.mjs --url http://localhost:9211 --runs 3 --dir ~/receipts-eval --truth ~/receipts-eval/truth.json --only r1,r2,r3,r4,r5,r6,r7,r8` reads `ocr/<id>.json` for the OCR lines. Report the table by receipt id only (preferences 53).
- **Fall back from flex.** Add `SCAN_PASS1_FLEX_DEADLINE_MS=1` to `.dev.vars`, relaunch, and scan once with `--runs 1 --only t1`. `.verify/9211/wrangler.log` shows a `"event":"scan"` line with `"path":{"pass1":"standard",...}`, a first call with `"step":"flex"` and `"outcome":"timeout"`, and a second with `"step":"standard"` and `"host":"Google"`. The route counts the abandoned flex call at `SCAN_PASS1_FLEX_BOUND_USD`, so `x-scan-cost` shows about $0.005 plus the Vertex call's cost. Remove the line and relaunch afterwards.
- **A refusal.** `curl -s -X POST http://localhost:9211/api/scan -H 'origin: https://other.example' -d '{}'` prints `{"refused":"origin"}`.

## Gotchas

- `npm run e2e` with `.dev.vars` in the worktree sends every scan in the suite to OpenRouter and spends money. Move `.dev.vars` aside before the suite.
- OCR takes about 0.4 to 1 s once the models are cached, so the reading state can be gone before a slow assertion runs. Hold a model download with `page.route` to keep it on screen.
- `react-native-web` compiles `animationKeyframes` only inside `StyleSheet.create`. An inline keyframe style renders with `animation-name: none`.
- The OCR worker stays alive for 10 s after the last scan, then the page terminates it. A scan inside that window reuses the engine.
- The service worker answers only while `navigator.onLine` is false, so Playwright routes still see every online request. Offline it serves only what its caches hold, so a route it never kept, such as `/s/<id>`, fails as it would without it.
- To switch the service worker off in production, set `KILL = true` at the top of `public/sw.js` and deploy. Browsers fetch `sw.js` past the HTTP cache (`_headers` serves it `no-cache`), and the new worker deletes every cache and unregisters. The page registers it again on each load and it removes itself again, so keep `KILL` set until a fixed worker ships.
- Local storage carries the draft between loads in one browser context. Start each check in a fresh context, as Playwright does per test.
- The Example has no photo, so its card shows the receipt glyph instead of a thumbnail.
- Below 768 px there is no **Example** link. A phone-width spec widens the page to 768 px for the tap with `openExample` in `e2e/files.ts`.
- `e2e/fixtures/make-fixtures.py` writes the fixtures it derives from `bench/data/srd-1040.jpg`, and draws the scan fixtures in `bench/scan/fixtures/` from their invented TSVs.
- The scan limits count per address in the lane's own Durable Object storage, and every local scan comes from `localhost`. A seventh scan in a minute gets 429, and the 21st in a day too. `run.mjs` waits out a `retry-after` of 65 s or less. For a harness run of more than 20 scans, add `SCAN_PER_MINUTE=100` and `SCAN_PER_DAY=1000` to `.dev.vars` and relaunch. Leave them out when checking the limits themselves.
- `wrangler dev` reads `.dev.vars` and the `wrangler.jsonc` vars only at start. Relaunch after changing either.
