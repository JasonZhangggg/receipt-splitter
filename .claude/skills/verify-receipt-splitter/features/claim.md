# Claim

The Split page `/s/<id>` shows the Receipt as a card and a bottom sheet. A device with no name stored opens the tall sheet "Which one are you?" over the card. It shows the invite line, such as "Jason invited you to split $28.04", or "Link ready." on the device that made the link. Names another device holds read **Picked**, and tapping one shows "Picked on another phone." with **That's me** and **Cancel**. **Not here? Add yourself** adds a name. After a pick the row shows a check for 80 ms, then the sheet glides down to the people chips as the rows fade out and the share line, **Your share** with the amount and an up-arrow, fades in, with your chip marked by a small person icon after the name (its accessible name still reads "<name>, you"). The ✕ or a flick down anywhere on the tall sheet closes it without a pick, and the page browses (frame K). The share bar folds the breakdown: a swipe up or a tap on **Your share** opens it, and a swipe down, a tap on the row, or a tap on your chip folds it. The **Your share** row is one element that moves from under the chips to under the Item list while the sheet rises, the list fades in with the same progress, the pay buttons fade in where they rest over the last tenth of the way, and the arrow turns to point down once the sheet is past halfway. Open, the chips, the Items count, the Adjustments, the total and the pay buttons hold still and only the Item list scrolls. The list reaches out into the sheet's gutter, so its scroll bar sits 22 px clear of the prices. List text is 14 px, the same as the card's rows. The breakdown never counts unclaimed lines. A breakdown with no Items shows one gray row in the list, "Check off what you had." for your own and "Nothing checked for Sam." for anyone else's, so it is as tall as one with one Item and folds over the same 86 px. `e2e/sheet.spec.ts` drives these gestures on WebKit (`mobile-safari`, iPhone 15) and Chromium; real iOS touch arbitration still needs a phone. Each row is one line of the receipt. A line printed with a quantity is one row with "3x" before its name. The circle on the left of a row checks the whole line for the selected chip, and the rest of the row opens the Item sheet for that line: name, price, **Who had it** with **+**, and **Evenly**, **By shares**, and **By amount**, all for the whole line. A quantity line's sheet also shows its **Quantity** and **Split into 3**, as [receipt.md](receipt.md) describes. Edits save as you go. The circle is always the selected chip's own check: filled with a tick when that person had the line, empty otherwise, and the caption above the rows says whose it is ("Check what you had", or "Check what **Sam** had" with the name in ink). Everyone else who had the line shows as small gray initials before the price, in table order, three at most and then "+N" (test id `claimer-marks`; a checked circle has the test id `checked`). An unclaimed row shows an empty circle and no initials, the Unclaimed line sits under the Total and counts the lines nobody fully had, such as "Unclaimed · 3 items". A quantity line counts once, and a By amount line with money left over counts as one. "Items are $0.25 over." shows while the Items disagree with the printed subtotal. Tapping a chip shows that person's breakdown, which lists one line per row on the card, a quantity line with its "3x" and never "1 of 3", your own under **Your share**, **Pay <Host> ... on <app>**, and **I already paid**. The pay buttons show only in the expanded breakdown, never on the share line. **More** ("⋯") in the header holds **Payments** (who has paid, see [pay.md](pay.md)), **Receipt photo** while the Split has one, and **History**. **History** opens History, which lists Payments and Receipt changes with **Revert**, asks first before reverting another device's change, and offers **Restore** on "Created the split".

## Sub-features

- `claim-pick` picks a name on the tall sheet and collapses it to the chips, with "Link ready." for the creating device. The tall sheet ends with one link row: the link cut in the middle, a **Copy** icon button, and a **Share** icon button where `navigator.share` exists.
- `claim-take` moves a name picked on another phone with **That's me**, and drops the other phone back to browsing.
- `claim-browse` closes the tall sheet without a pick, and a tap on a circle asks again.
- `claim-check` checks a line for the selected chip, and every browser shows the claimers and Shares.
- `claim-item` edits the name, price, who had the line, and the split mode in the Item sheet, and **Delete item** removes the line.
- `claim-breakdown` shows a person's Items (or the gray empty row when they have none), their parts of each Adjustment, their total, and one gray line under the share (see [pay.md](./pay.md)). On the phone it folds into the share bar, and on desktop it fills the right pane. In both, the Items and the Adjustment rows under them scroll as one list. The Items have no hairlines between them; one hairline sits above the Adjustments and one above the share row. Only the people, the share row and the pay pills stay still. `e2e/sheet.spec.ts` checks that with two ways to pay on a 375 by 667 phone the list is at least three rows (138 px) tall and reaches Tip by scrolling.
- `claim-paid` marks the payer paid with **I already paid**.
- `claim-history` lists Payments and Receipt changes, reverts one with a confirmation for another device's change, and restores the Receipt.
- `claim-people` keeps the people chips on one row that scrolls sideways, edge to edge of the sheet or pane, with the first chip on the gutter at rest. The selected chip is ink with white text. Every other chip, picked or not, and the **+** share one light gray fill with ink text and no outline. No chip shows a paid mark; Payments in the More menu holds that. A long-press (right-click on desktop) on anyone but the Host opens a small menu with **Remove <name>** and, when they checked Items or paid, a gray line such as "Removing Priya unchecks 1 item." Removing them unchecks their Items (a co-claimer takes the whole Item), clears their Payment and frees their phone, which drops back to browsing. History shows "Removed <name> from the split" with **Revert**. `e2e/people.spec.ts` drives the hold, the removal and a 12-name row.
- `claim-mismatch` shows a muted note above the Subtotal while the Total check fails, such as "Items are $0.25 over the subtotal." It blocks nothing.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a Split link, or tap **Create split link** on `/`, which opens the Split page for the Host.
- Tap a name on the tall sheet, or **Not here? Add yourself**, or the ✕.
- Tap a line's circle, or the rest of the line.
- Tap a chip, or "Your share".
- Tap the clock icon in the header.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

One `drive` opens one browser and cannot pick or tap, so every step below runs as a Playwright spec or a short Playwright script against the lane's port. `e2e/claim.spec.ts` is the reference. Run it with `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/claim.spec.ts`, which builds and starts its own `wrangler dev`.

- **A Split with a known Receipt.** `POST http://localhost:8811/api/splits` with `{"split":{"receipt":...,"participants":[{"id":"jason","name":"Jason"},{"id":"sam","name":"Sam"}],"paymentHandles":[{"service":"venmo","handle":"jason-z"}]}}` returns 201 with the `id`. An Item may carry `"quantity": 2`. Send a different `cf-connecting-ip` header on each create, because each IP may create 10 Splits a minute and 50 per UTC day. All IPs together may create 1,000 Splits per UTC day.
- **Pick (`claim-pick`).** Open the link in a fresh context. The dialog named `Which one are you?` lists the names. Tap the button with the exact name. The chip `<name>, you` shows within half a second. Save `<out>/claim-pick.png`.
- **That's me (`claim-take`).** In a second context, tap `<name>, picked on another phone`, then **That's me**. The second page shows `<name>, you`, and the first shows "Pick your name to check off what you had."
- **Browse (`claim-browse`).** Tap **Close, just look**. The prompt and the pill **Which one are you?** show. Tapping a circle, named `Who had <Item>`, reopens the tall sheet.
- **Check (`claim-check`).** A circle is a toggle button named `<Item>, check for <name>` with `aria-pressed`. The rest of the line is a button named `Open <Item>, $<price>, had by <names>` or `..., nobody had it`. A quantity line puts its quantity before the name in both, such as `Open 2x Pitchers, $24.00, nobody had it`. The test ids are `owe` for your total on the folded chips sheet (the same element is `share` once the sheet opens), `breakdown-items` for the part of the breakdown that fades, `share-arrow` for its arrow, `unclaimed-count` for the Unclaimed line's label with its count, `unclaimed` and `total` on the card, `share-note` for the paid line under a share, `share` and `breakdown` in a breakdown, and `line-<id>` for a line.
- **Item sheet (`claim-item`).** The dialog named `Item` has the textboxes `Item name` and `Price`, the line's full price, a group `Who had it` of toggle buttons per person, and the toggle buttons **Evenly**, **By shares**, and **By amount**. Shares have **More shares for <name>** and **Fewer shares for <name>**. Amounts have the textbox `<name>'s amount`, empty with a gray `0.00` placeholder until typed, and the test id `amount-status` under the switch reads "$62.75 left to assign", "All $87.75 assigned" or "$7.25 over the price" with "Not saved". While exactly one person is at $0.00 and money is left, the button **Give <name> the rest, $<left>** writes the remainder into that field. A burst of edits goes 600 ms after the last one, or when the sheet closes, as one History row.
- **People (`claim-people`).** The chips are buttons in the group `People`. On the phone a hold needs a real touch: `e2e/people.spec.ts` sends one through CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` from the test's own page (a plain `newContext` has no touch screen). A right-click opens the same menu, the dialog `Options for <name>`, with the button **Remove <name>**.
- **History (`claim-history`).** Tap **More**, then **History**. Each row is a list item with **Revert <Item>**. A change sent under another name asks `Revert <name>'s change?` in an alertdialog. **Restore** on "Created the split" asks "Put the receipt back as it was created?".
- **Live flow.** `PORT=8811 OUT=<out> node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/split-flow.mjs` drives the main flow with four browsers at 390 px: the Host creates a Split, two browsers pick, claim, split Tostones 2:1, claim Perrier from its Item sheet, change Sprite to $2.75 while a third browser's socket is down for 10 seconds, the payer marks paid, the Host reverts the price after the confirmation, a reload keeps the pick, and a fourth browser takes Sam with **That's me**. It saves each board frame as `<out>/<frame>.png` and writes `<out>/report.json` with the checks, the claim-to-other-browser time, the reconnect times, the properties animating mid-collapse, and console errors. Add `SCHEME=dark`, `REDUCE=1`, or `VIDEO=1` for dark mode, reduced motion, or a recording in `<out>/video/`.

## Gotchas

- The page shows no merchant name, because the Receipt keeps none.
- Claims are not recorded in History, so a cleared Claim comes back only by checking it again.
- The pick sheet stays tall, with its top 170 px from the top of the window, even when it lists few names.
- A device keeps its pick per Split in local storage with its own device id. A fresh browser context is a new device, so a reload in the same context keeps the pick and a new context does not.
- `context.setOffline(true)` does not close an open WebSocket. Use `page.routeWebSocket` to drop one, as [live.md](./live.md) says.
