# Receipt

The receipt card on the Split page `/s/<id>` lists the Receipt as the receipt prints it, grouped by Bundle. Lines printed separately stay separate rows. A line printed with a quantity is one row with "3x" before its name, in the gray caption type of a Bundle's "6 items" count, at the line's full price, so Guinness x3 reads as one row "3x Guinness Draught" at 21.87. Its circle and its Item sheet act on the whole line. The column heads read **Item** and **Price**. No line shows an ×. A Bundle, such as Topgolf's "Beer Bucket", is one row on the card's surface with the Bundle's name, a summary such as "6 items · 10% off", its net price, and a chevron. It has no claim circle. Every Bundle starts collapsed. Closed, it shows a dot for each person who had something inside it, and a gray "1 unclaimed" once claiming has started and a unit inside it has no claimer. Open, it lists one row per line, indented so each row's circle lines up with the left edge of the Bundle's name, with a light vertical line from under the header's empty leading slot down beside them, then its discount, such as "Discount (10%) -4.51". The receipt-level Adjustments (Tax, Tip) stay under the Subtotal. A Bundle's discount shows in a breakdown only for a person who had an Item in that Bundle. Tapping a row opens its Item sheet, which for a Bundle Item adds a line such as "In Beer Bucket. Its 10% off takes $2.19 off this line." and ends with **Delete item**. A quantity line's sheet shows its **Quantity** under the price and a **Split into 3** button with a fork icon before the label. **Split** closes the sheet and turns the line into three one-unit rows at 7.29 inside the Bundle, each with the line's claims. The new rows open below the first row over about 200 ms while the rows under them slide down, and under reduced motion they fade in without the height change. History reads "Split Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can into 3", and **Revert** brings back the one "3x" row.

## Sub-features

- `receipt-quantity` shows a quantity line as one row with "3x" at its full price, claimed whole.
- `receipt-split` turns a quantity line into one-unit rows from its sheet for every browser, with a History row that **Revert** undoes.
- `receipt-bundle` opens and closes a Bundle in place by tap, Enter, or Space, with `aria-expanded`, and keeps focus on its header.
- `receipt-bundle-flag` shows a closed Bundle's unclaimed units in gray. On the first claim the line grows in over 200 ms, and on the last it collapses out over 150 ms, so the rows under it glide. Opening or closing the Bundle brings or removes it at once.
- `receipt-bundle-note` names the Bundle's discount on a Bundle Item's sheet.
- `receipt-delete` deletes an Item from its sheet for every browser, with a History row that **Revert** undoes.
- `receipt-adjustments` fixes, deletes or adds a tax, tip, fee or discount row, Bundle discounts included, for every browser, with the Total kept at the Subtotal plus the Adjustments and a History row that **Revert** undoes.
- `receipt-desktop` lays the page out from 1024 px as a centered pair, the receipt beside a right pane, with the Item detail, Add item and History as popovers.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a Split whose Receipt holds a Bundle, such as one made from `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` on `/`.
- Tap a Bundle's header, or focus it and press Enter or Space.
- Tap a line inside an open Bundle, then **Split into 3** or **Delete item**.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

One `drive` cannot tap, so these steps run as a Playwright spec or a short Playwright script against the lane's port. `e2e/receipt.spec.ts` is the reference. Run it with `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/receipt.spec.ts`.

- **A Topgolf Split.** `POST /api/splits` with the Receipt in `e2e/receipt.spec.ts` (`topgolf`), which is the Receipt that scanning `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` produces: Gameplay $87.75, two Beer Buckets with `bundleDiscount` rows of -4.51 and -4.63, Tax $4.94, and Tip $32.26, for $207.29.
- **Read a Bundle (`receipt-bundle`).** The header is a button named `Beer Bucket, 6 items, 10% off, collapsed`, with `aria-expanded="false"` and `aria-controls` naming the region of its Items. Press Enter on it. The name ends in `expanded`, focus stays on it, and the region holds one button named `Open 3x Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, $21.87, nobody had it`. A closed region holds no list. Once someone claims, the header name adds who had what and the count, such as `Beer Bucket, 6 items, 10% off, had by Sam, 3 unclaimed, collapsed`. Space closes it. Save an ARIA snapshot of the card with `page.getByTestId("receipt").ariaSnapshot()`.
- **Flag (`receipt-bundle-flag`).** Pick Sam and check Gameplay. Each closed header shows its unclaimed units in gray, "6 unclaimed" on the first Bundle. A closed Bundle shows nothing unclaimed before any line is checked.
- **Split (`receipt-quantity`, `receipt-split`, `receipt-bundle-note`).** Open the first Bundle. Its Guinness row is the button `Open 3x Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, $21.87, ...`, and its circle `3x Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, check for Sam` claims the whole line. Tap the row. The dialog named `Item` reads "In Beer Bucket. Its 10% off takes $2.19 off this line.", its `Price` reads 21.87, the test id `quantity` reads 3, and it has a button **Split into 3**. Tap it. The sheet closes and three buttons `Open Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, $7.29, ...` sit in the Bundle with the line's claims, in every browser. History has **Revert Split Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can into 3**. Reverting it from another browser asks `Revert Sam's change?`, then the one "3x" row comes back.
- **Delete (`receipt-delete`).** Open a one-unit Item such as the Michelob Ultra. Its sheet has no **Split** button. Tap **Delete item**. The header reads `Beer Bucket, 5 items, 10% off, ...` in every browser, and History reads "Removed Michelob Ultra 16oz Aluminum Bottle and 1 more", because the delete also edits the Bundle. Its button **Revert Removed Michelob Ultra 16oz Aluminum Bottle** brings it back.
- **Add item (`receipt-add`).** The last row of the card is the button **Add item**, above the Total check note and Subtotal. Tap it. The dialog named `New item` has the textboxes `Item name`, `Detail`, and `Price`, a quantity stepper (`One fewer`, `One more`), the group `Who had it`, and the button **Add item**. With no name, the button focuses `Item name` and shows "Add a name." Return walks from the name to the detail to the price, and Return in the price adds. Fill "Fries" and "6.50", turn on Sam, and add. The sheet closes and the button `Open Fries, $6.50, had by Sam` opens in at the end of the list, outside every Bundle, in every browser. History has **Revert Added Fries**, and from another browser it asks first. A Split at 100 Items shows "A split holds at most 100 items." and sends nothing. `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/add-item.spec.ts` covers these, and `node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/add-item-shots.mjs <port> <out>` saves the screenshot matrix at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark: the receipt, the empty sheet, "Add a name.", the filled sheet, and the added row.
- **Adjustments (`receipt-adjustments`).** Every row under **Subtotal** is a button named `Open <name>, <amount>`, such as `Open Tax, $4.94` or `Open Coupon, -$5.00`, on a 44 px tap target at the 34 px row pitch, and an open Bundle's discount row is one too (`Open Discount (10%), -$3.66`). A tap opens the dialog named `Charge` or `Discount` (sheet on a phone, popover from 1024 px). It holds the group `Kind` (**Tax**, **Tip**, **Fee**, **Discount**), the textbox `Name`, the textbox `Amount` (or `Amount off` after a "-$" for a discount, typed as a positive number), and **Delete charge** or **Delete discount**. It saves 600 ms after typing stops and on close, like the Item sheet. A Bundle discount's sheet has no `Kind` group and says "This discount comes off the 3 items in Beer Bucket." Switching a tax to **Discount** flips the stored sign, and a name still the old kind's word ("Tax") becomes the new one ("Discount"); a custom name stays. An amount typed as `4` reads `4.00` once the field is left. An edit the Split refuses, such as a discount that takes the Total under $0.00, keeps the sheet open through Close, Escape, a scrim tap and a swipe down, with the problem line ("That takes the total under $0.00.") and the typed amount. Nothing moves focus there except Close, which gives it back to the field it took it from, so the keyboard stays up only if it was up. After a field's Cut/Copy/Paste menu on iOS, a swipe down still moves and closes the sheet. Every save sends `totalFollows`, so the Total becomes the Subtotal plus the Adjustments: a fixed tip moves it by the change, and fixing a misread tax clears the gray Total check note. The last row of the block, **Add tax, tip or discount**, opens the dialog of that name with the same fields, the first of Tax and Tip the receipt lacks already chosen, and a button **Add tip** (or **Add tax**, **Add fee**, **Add discount**). An empty name takes the kind's name; no amount says "Add an amount." and focuses `Amount`. The new row grows in under the others in every browser, and a deleted row collapses out over 150 ms (a fade under reduced motion); the motion scenario `charge-delete` frame-checks it. History lists "Tax 4.94 → 6.00", "Added Tip" or "Removed Coupon", and Revert from another browser asks first. `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/adjustments.spec.ts` covers these, and `node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/adjustment-shots.mjs <port> <out>` saves the matrix at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark: the receipt, the tax sheet, the fixed tax, the discount and Bundle discount sheets, the add sheet, "Add an amount.", the added tip, and the breakdown.
- **Desktop (`receipt-desktop`).** At 1024 px and wider, the header spans a centered wrap of up to 1064 px. Under it the receipt sits on the left and the right pane, `role="complementary"` named `People`, sits on the right at 400 px, level with the receipt's top. The pane sticks 16 px from the top while the receipt scrolls. Before this browser picks, the pane holds the pick content: the invite line, the level-2 heading "Which one are you?" (the wordmark is the page's only level-1 heading), the people, **Not here? Add yourself**, and the link row, with no close button. After a pick it holds the people chips and the selected person's breakdown with the pay button, and no link row. A chip shows that person's breakdown, and the selected chip goes back to yours. A row's circle before a pick moves focus to the pane's first person. A click on a row opens the dialog named `Item` 6 px under the line, its right edge 14 px past the line's, and it flips above the line when there is more room there. **Add item** opens `New item` and the header's **History** opens `History` the same way. Escape, a click outside, or a width change closes a popover, and focus goes back to what opened it. When Split, Delete item, or another device takes the Item away, the popover or sheet closes showing the Item as it was, and focus goes to the nearest line still on screen, the next one first. A popover opened with Enter shows at once; a click scales it in from 0.96 over 200 ms. Under 1024 px the page keeps its bottom sheets, and they also close on Escape. `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/desktop.spec.ts --project=mobile-chrome` covers these, also with `SLOW_RAF=200`. `node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/desktop-shots.mjs <port> <out>` saves the pick, popover, open Bundle, History and Add item frames at 1440 and 1024 px in light and dark, plus 390 px phone frames, and prints the popover animation counts.

## Gotchas

- The summary counts units, not lines. The first Bundle has four lines and six units.
- A Bundle's Items render only while it is open, so open it before looking for a line inside it.
- The header's net price is the Items' sum plus the discount, so it is not any printed line on the receipt.
- The rows Split adds take the ids `<id>-2`, `<id>-3`, so their test ids are `line-guinness1-2` and `line-guinness1-3`.
- Screenshot the matrix at 390 px and 1440 px, in light and dark. The desktop layout also needs 1024 px, its narrowest width.
- Under React Native Web a `role="button"` target is a native `<button>`, so Enter reaches `onPress` as a click whose `detail` is 0. That is how the page tells a keyboard open from a click.
- An add sends no Subtotal or Total change. A receipt whose printed Subtotal already counts the missing line clears its Total check note after the add; any other add opens or grows the note.
