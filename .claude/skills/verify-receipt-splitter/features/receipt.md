# Receipt

The Items list on the Split page `/s/<id>` lists the Receipt as the receipt prints it, grouped by Bundle, under an "Items" heading with the viewer's count on the right (`items-count`, such as "4 of 7 yours" or "2 of 7 Sam's"). Each row is a 12 px dot that claims it (`checked` test id when the viewer has it) and a main button that opens the Item sheet. The left column is the name, the detail, then who had it ("You, Sam", this phone's owner first, or "Nobody yet"); the right column is the full price and, under it, the split line ("$4.67 yours" for the viewer, "$6.25 Sam's" when viewing Sam, "$6.25 each", "by shares", "by amount"). Gray hairlines sit between rows. Lines printed separately stay separate rows. A line printed with a quantity is one row with "3x" before its name, in the gray caption type of a Bundle's "4 items" count, at the line's full price, so Guinness x3 reads as one row "3x Guinness Draught" at 21.87. Its circle and its Item sheet act on the whole line. No line shows an ×. A Bundle, such as Topgolf's "Beer Bucket", is one row on the card's surface with the Bundle's name, a summary such as "4 items · 10% off", counting rows, so a 3x line is one, its net price, and a chevron. It has no claim dot; its chevron sits in the dot column. Every Bundle starts collapsed. Closed, it shows who had something inside it under its summary and a gray "1 claimed" on the right, counting its rows that have a claimer. A row nobody had, or a By amount row with money left over, is not claimed, the same rows the Unclaimed line under the Total counts as unclaimed. With none claimed it shows nothing. Open, it lists one row per line, indented so each row's circle lines up with the left edge of the Bundle's name, with a light vertical line from under the header's empty leading slot down beside them and no hairlines between them, then its discount, such as "Discount (10%) -4.51". The receipt-level Adjustments (Tax, Tip) stay under the Subtotal. A Bundle's discount shows in a breakdown only for a person who had an Item in that Bundle. Tapping a row opens its Item sheet, which for a Bundle Item adds a line such as "In Beer Bucket. Its 10% off takes $2.19 off this line." and has the trash icon (**Delete item**) in its header. A quantity line's sheet shows its **Quantity** under the price and a **Split into 3** button with a fork icon before the label. **Split** closes the sheet and turns the line into three one-unit rows at 7.29 inside the Bundle, each with the line's claims. The new rows open below the first row over about 200 ms while the rows under them slide down, and under reduced motion they fade in without the height change. History reads "Split Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can into 3", and **Revert** brings back the one "3x" row.

## Sub-features

- `receipt-quantity` shows a quantity line as one row with "3x" at its full price, claimed whole.
- `receipt-split` turns a quantity line into one-unit rows from its sheet for every browser, with a History row that **Revert** undoes.
- `receipt-bundle` opens and closes a Bundle in place by tap, Enter, or Space, with `aria-expanded`, and keeps focus on its header.
- `receipt-bundle-flag` shows a closed Bundle's claimed Items in gray. On the first claim the line grows in over 200 ms, and on the last it collapses out over 150 ms, so the rows under it glide. Opening or closing the Bundle brings or removes it at once.
- `receipt-bundle-note` names the Bundle's discount on a Bundle Item's sheet.
- `receipt-delete` deletes an Item from its sheet for every browser, with a History row that **Revert** undoes.
- `receipt-adjustments` fixes, deletes or adds a tax, tip, fee or discount row, Bundle discounts included, for every browser, with the Total kept at the Subtotal plus the Adjustments and a History row that **Revert** undoes.
- `receipt-desktop` keeps one centered column of up to 480 px from 1024 px up, with the footer fixed under it and every sheet rising from the bottom.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a Split whose Receipt holds a Bundle, such as one made from `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` on `/`.
- Tap a Bundle's header, or focus it and press Enter or Space.
- Tap a line inside an open Bundle, then **Split into 3** or **Delete item**.

## Driving it with verify.mjs

Preconditions:

- `verify.mjs doctor --port 8811` reports `"ok":true`.
- `<out>` is the lane's output folder, as defined in [README.md](./README.md).

One `drive` cannot tap, so these steps run as a Playwright spec or a short Playwright script against the lane's port. `e2e/receipt.spec.ts` is the reference. Run it with `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/receipt.spec.ts`.

- **A Topgolf Split.** `POST /api/splits` with the Receipt in `e2e/receipt.spec.ts` (`topgolf`), which is the Receipt that scanning `e2e/fixtures/topgolf-synthetic.png` produces: Gameplay $87.75, two Beer Buckets with `bundleDiscount` rows of -4.51 and -4.63, Tax $4.94, and Tip $32.26, for $207.29.
- **Read a Bundle (`receipt-bundle`).** The header is a button named `Beer Bucket, 4 items, 10% off, collapsed`, with `aria-expanded="false"` and `aria-controls` naming the region of its Items. Press Enter on it. The name ends in `expanded`, focus stays on it, and the region holds one button named `Open 3x Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, $21.87, nobody had it`. A closed region holds no list. Once someone claims, the header name adds who had what and the count, such as `Beer Bucket, 4 items, 10% off, had by Sam, 1 claimed, collapsed`. Space closes it. Save an ARIA snapshot of the card with `page.getByTestId("receipt").ariaSnapshot()`.
- **Flag (`receipt-bundle-flag`).** Pick Sam, open the first Bundle and check the 3x Guinness line, then close it. Its header shows Sam's dot and "1 claimed" in gray. The second Bundle shows nothing, and so does any closed Bundle before a line in it is checked.
- **Split (`receipt-quantity`, `receipt-split`, `receipt-bundle-note`).** Open the first Bundle. Its Guinness row is the button `Open 3x Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, $21.87, ...`, and its circle `3x Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, check for Sam` claims the whole line. Tap the row. The dialog named `Item` reads "In Beer Bucket. Its 10% off takes $2.19 off this line.", its `Price` reads 21.87, the test id `quantity` reads 3, and it has a button **Split into 3**. Tap it. The sheet closes and three buttons `Open Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can, $7.29, ...` sit in the Bundle with the line's claims, in every browser. History has **Revert Split Guinness Draught 14.9oz Can into 3**. Reverting it from another browser asks `Revert Sam's change?`, then the one "3x" row comes back.
- **Delete (`receipt-delete`).** Open a one-unit Item such as the Michelob Ultra. Its sheet has no **Split** button. Tap the trash icon (**Delete item**). The Total (`total`) drops by the Item's price, and **Undo** puts it back. The bar reads "Deleted Michelob Ultra 16oz Aluminum Bottle · Undo" and the header reads `Beer Bucket, 5 items, 10% off, ...` in every browser, and History reads "Removed Michelob Ultra 16oz Aluminum Bottle and 1 more", because the delete also edits the Bundle. Its button **Revert Removed Michelob Ultra 16oz Aluminum Bottle** brings it back. The deleted row, at the top level, inside an open Bundle, or one unit of a Split line, stays in place showing what it showed and collapses out over 150 ms while the rows under it slide up (a fade under reduced motion), the same exit as a deleted charge. A delete from another phone does the same. Deleting a Bundle's last line takes the Bundle with it, and the Bundle collapses out as one row. A Revert grows the row back in. The motion scenarios `item-delete`, `bundle-item-delete` and `reduced-item-delete` frame-check it.
- **Add item (`receipt-add`).** Under the charges is the underlined link **Add item, tax, tip or discount**. Tap it for the one dialog named `Add to the receipt`: the group `What to add` (**Item**, **Tax**, **Tip**, **Fee**, **Discount**, underlined words, **Item** chosen), the textboxes `Name`, `Price` and `How many` (Item only, empty reads as 1; the price is the whole line's, and 1 to 20 is allowed, otherwise "Use a whole number from 1 to 20."), the hint "Split by each person who had it." and the button **Add item**. With tax or tip on the Split, Item also shows "Tax and tip stay at the receipt's amounts and are shared across the new item too." (or the tax-only or tip-only sentence). When the Items are short of the printed Subtotal, Item shows "The receipt is $6.50 short." and the underlined button **Use $6.50**, which fills `Price`. With no name, the button focuses `Name` and shows "Add a name." Return walks from the name to the price to How many, and Return in How many adds. Fill "Fries" and "6.50" and add. The sheet closes and the button `Open Fries, $6.50, Nobody yet` opens in at the end of the list, outside every Bundle, in every browser, and the Total (`total`) rises by $6.50 with no Total check note. How many 3 with "21.87" adds `Open 3x Guinness, $21.87, ...`, a quantity line whose Edit item sheet has **Split into 3**; tap its dot or open it to say who had it, and add a detail in its Edit item sheet. History has **Revert Added Fries**, and from another browser it asks first. A Split at 100 Items shows "A split holds at most 100 items." and sends nothing. `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/add-item.spec.ts` covers these, and `node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/add-item-shots.mjs <port> <out>` saves the screenshot matrix at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark: the receipt, the empty sheet, "Add a name.", the filled sheet, and the added row.
- **Adjustments (`receipt-adjustments`).** Every row under **Subtotal** is a button named `Open <name>, <amount>`, such as `Open Tax, $4.94` or `Open Coupon, -$5.00`, on a 44 px tap target at the 34 px row pitch, and an open Bundle's discount row is one too (`Open Discount (10%), -$3.66`). A tap opens the dialog named `Charge` or `Discount`. It holds the group `What to add` (**Tax**, **Tip**, **Fee**, **Discount**), the textbox `Name`, the textbox `Amount` (or `Amount off` after a "-$" for a discount, typed as a positive number), and the trash icon **Delete charge** or **Delete discount** in the header, which shows the bar "Deleted <name> · Undo". It saves 600 ms after typing stops and on close, like the Item sheet. A Bundle discount's sheet has no `What to add` group and says "This discount comes off the 3 items in Beer Bucket." Switching a tax to **Discount** flips the stored sign, and a name still the old kind's word ("Tax") becomes the new one ("Discount"); a custom name stays. An amount typed as `4` reads `4.00` once the field is left. An edit the Split refuses, such as a discount that takes the Total under $0.00, keeps the sheet open through Close, Escape, a scrim tap and a swipe down, with the problem line ("That takes the total under $0.00.") and the typed amount. None of these moves focus. Pressing Close leaves a focused field focused, so the keyboard and the field stay where they are. After a field's Cut/Copy/Paste menu on iOS, a swipe down still moves and closes the sheet. Every save sends `totalFollows`, so the Total becomes the Subtotal plus the Adjustments: a fixed tip moves it by the change, and fixing a misread tax clears the gray Total check note. The receipt has one **Add item, tax, tip or discount** link under the charges; in the `Add to the receipt` dialog choosing **Tax**, **Tip**, **Fee** or **Discount** keeps the typed name and amount and shows the button **Add tip** (or **Add tax**, **Add fee**, **Add discount**). An empty name takes the kind's name; no amount says "Add an amount." and focuses `Amount`. The new row grows in under the others in every browser, and a deleted row collapses out over 150 ms (a fade under reduced motion); the motion scenario `charge-delete` frame-checks it. History lists "Tax 4.94 → 6.00", "Added Tip" or "Removed Coupon", and Revert from another browser asks first. `PORT=<port> npx playwright test e2e/adjustments.spec.ts` covers these, and `node .claude/skills/verify-receipt-splitter/adjustment-shots.mjs <port> <out>` saves the matrix at 390 and 1440 px, light and dark: the receipt, the tax sheet, the fixed tax, the discount and Bundle discount sheets, the add sheet, "Add an amount.", the added tip, and the breakdown.
- **Layout (`receipt-desktop`).** Phone and desktop share one centered column of up to 480 px: the header (wordmark as Home, the sun/moon theme toggle, **Share link**), the intro line ("<Host> paid $X. Tap the dot next to what you had. Tap a line to edit it or split it another way."), the underlined links **Receipt** (only while the Split has a photo), **People**, **Payments** and **History**, then the receipt. A fixed footer shows "Your share" (or "<name>'s share"), the amount (`owe`), "N of M items · incl. tax & tip" (or the paid line, `share-note`) and the **Pay <Host first name>** pill; the Host and a person viewed from someone else's side get no pill. Before a name is picked the footer holds "Pick your name..." and **Which one are you?**. Every sheet is a bottom sheet at every width, and Escape closes it. **Add item, tax, tip or discount** opens the Add sheet. Resize a Playwright page across 1024 px to check the column and the footer keep their state.

## Gotchas

- The summary counts units, not lines. The first Bundle has four lines and six units.
- A Bundle's Items render only while it is open, so open it before looking for a line inside it.
- The header's net price is the Items' sum plus the discount, so it is not any printed line on the receipt.
- The rows Split adds take the ids `<id>-2`, `<id>-3`, so their test ids are `line-guinness1-2` and `line-guinness1-3`.
- Screenshot the matrix at 390 px and 1440 px, in light and dark. The desktop layout also needs 1024 px, its narrowest width.
- Under React Native Web a `role="button"` target is a native `<button>`, so Enter reaches `onPress` as a click whose `detail` is 0. That is how the page tells a keyboard open from a click.
- An Item add, delete or price edit moves the Subtotal and the Total in the operation itself (preferences 70); the client sends no delta. An added Item first fills any shortfall of the Items under the printed Subtotal (a line the reading missed), and only the rest raises the Subtotal and Total. On Rice $8.00 against a printed $14.50, adding $6.50 leaves the Total at $14.50 and clears the note, and adding $10.00 raises it by $3.50. A price edit or a delete moves the Subtotal and Total by the full difference, gap or not, so a price taken down and back up returns to the Total it started at. A delete that would take the Total under $0.00 is refused. Printed tax, tip and fees keep their amounts. A delete that empties a Bundle also takes its discount off the Total. Undo and Revert negate the recorded `totals` write, so they give back exactly what the change moved, even after other phones' edits.
